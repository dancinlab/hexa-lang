F-FUSION-H1B-MEGAKERNEL  ::  device-resident full-step MEGAKERNEL SKELETON (option-B, B1)
domain: HEXA-FUSION  ·  date: 2026-06-05  ·  method: cooperative-launch persistent kernel + own-GEMM inline + sub-chain rel-RMS A/B + util A/B

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRE-REGISTERED FALSIFIER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A persistent grid-resident megakernel with INLINE own-GEMM (no cuBLAS) runs a
GEMM → glue(gelu) → GEMM sub-chain in ONE cooperative launch, producing output
that matches the EAGER same-sub-chain (own-GEMM 3-launch path) within the TF32
own-GEMM bar (rel-RMS <= 3e-3), AND raises GPU util on the SAME sub-chain vs the
eager realization (collapsing the two launch-gaps lifts util).

FALSIFIED if: (a) the cooperative launch cannot be co-resident (occupancy=0 /
register or smem over-subscription), OR (b) [MEGASTEP-FIRED] never fires, OR
(c) rel-RMS > 3e-3 on the sub-chain, OR (d) util(megakernel) <= util(eager) on
the SAME sub-chain (the launch-gap collapse buys no occupancy).

SCOPE (honest): this is a SKELETON — the smallest end-to-end witness (GEMM→gelu→
GEMM), NOT the full ~30-op clm_prod step. NO "full megakernel done" claim is made
or implied. This verdict establishes whether the STRUCTURE runs at all and what
remains for the full step.

WHY NOW UNBLOCKED: F-FUSION-MEGAKERNEL-DESIGN ruled the FULL megakernel CLOSED-
NEGATIVE *for the FP64 + cuBLAS-roofline workload* — a persistent kernel cannot
call cublasDgemm (nested grid launch), and a hand FP64 GEMM breaks byte-eq +
forfeits cuBLAS throughput. The own-GEMM stack (#2697 tiled · #2704 WMMA2 ·
#2714 split-K) removes BOTH walls for the TF32 regime: the persistent kernel
calls OUR WMMA2 GEMM inline (no cuBLAS dependency), and the correctness bar is
the approved TF32 own-GEMM rel-RMS <= 3e-3, not FP64 byte-eq. The eager
reference ALSO uses own-GEMM, so the comparison is own-GEMM-vs-own-GEMM.

METHOD
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Artifacts (this branch):
  megakernel_driver.cu        — driver + _hx_k_clm_megastep (cooperative kernel)
  gemm_device_extracted.cuh   — _hx_k_sgemm_cm_wmma2 body as a __device__ fn
                                 (identical math; tile coord from params not blockIdx)
  build_megastep.sh           — extract own-GEMM kernels from the shipped .cu, nvcc -rdc=true
  util_megastep.sh            — external nvidia-smi util sampler, eager vs mega, SAME sub-chain
Structure demonstrated:
  (1) cudaLaunchCooperativeKernel + cooperative_groups::this_grid().sync() barriers
      between dependent phases (phase counter == the grid barrier).
  (2) inline own-GEMM tiles: WMMA2 body reused VERBATIM as a __device__ function;
      the persistent kernel calls OUR GEMM (the own-GEMM unblock).
  (3) gelu fused inline between the GEMM phases; intermediate H1/G kept device-
      resident across grid.sync() — no HBM-to-host round-trip, no relaunch gap.
Probe: [MEGASTEP-FIRED] printed once when the cooperative launch + grid.sync()
  path actually runs (env HEXA_CLM_MEGASTEP=1).
Sub-chain dims: M=1024 K=1536 N1=1536 N2=1024 (clm_prod conv-block-ish, D~1536).
Hardware: ONE H100 (vast, --owner h1b-mega), down-after, leak 0.
