VERDICT (CLOSED-NEGATIVE, host-removal axis):
  Removing the host from the per-step critical path — async (②), piecewise graph
  (④), whole-step graph (⑤) — caps util MEAN at ~13% on the H100 and does NOT
  reach the >=20% GREEN gate. Growing the workload (D) makes it WORSE under BOTH
  eager and graph. The binding constraint is NOT host launch overhead — it is GPU
  OCCUPANCY: the CLMConvMoE step is a chain of MANY SMALL, largely sequential
  kernels (norm / element-wise / MoE glue between the GEMMs) that under-fill the
  H100 (median util 2%, PEAK 71-100% only during the brief GEMMs). The H100 is
  too big for this model/T. Host-removal was necessary (④ recovers byte-eq that ②
  broke, the first correct lever) but is NOT sufficient for util-GREEN.
  Two genuine remaining levers to RAISE util:
   (A) KERNEL FUSION (the domain moat, L3 §5d) — merge the small inter-GEMM ops
       into fewer/larger kernels (or GEMM epilogues) to raise per-kernel occupancy.
   (B) RIGHT-SIZED GPU — the same workload saturates a smaller GPU (early RTX 5070
       showed ~90% util at D=1536); util is GPU-relative.
