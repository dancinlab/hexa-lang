RECONCILIATION with #2912 / #2913
─────────────────────────────────
  #2912 attributed the ~1656× torch gap to "the INTERPRETED per-step driver glue
  … running IN THE BYTECODE INTERPRETER between device GEMM spikes". This verdict
  FALSIFIES that specific attribution for the host-ARITHMETIC glue: native-
  compiling that glue gives ~1.0×, and the glue itself costs only ~0.17s/pass at
  B=1 (1690ms ÷ 10 reps) — NOT the ~5.98 s/step the real trainer takes. The real
  ~6 s/step "between-GEMM idle" is therefore dominated NOT by bytecode
  interpretation of arithmetic but by the SERIAL PER-OP STRUCTURE: ~58 separate
  FP64 cuBLAS GEMM + per-op device launches per step, each with launch/sync/D2H-
  H2D dispatch latency, plus FP64 (vs torch TF32). #2912's #2913 the wall is
  structural (serial un-fused op-DAG + FP64 + launch dispatch), already shown by
  the SIBLING closed-negatives M2/M3/M5 (more fusion / bigger D / bigger batch all
  fail to cross util-GREEN) — NOT removable by AOT-compiling the driver.

FINDING (closed-negative — ruled-out axis)
───────────────────────────────────────────
  🔴 FALSIFIED. Interpreter-elimination of the per-step driver does NOT uncap the
  flame CLMConvMoE step beyond the ≈3× batch-fill asymptote and closes ≈0% of the
  ~1656× torch gap. Native-compiling the per-step host-arithmetic glue (token-pack
  + im2col/col2im + db-colsum + CE-grad + AdamW) is byte-eq (max|Δ|=0) but gives
  ~1.0× — because the bytecode interpreter already dispatches every heavy element
  op (farr_get/farr_set, adamw_step, exp) as a native-C builtin; only the cheap
  loop scaffold is interpreted. The ~3× / ~1656× wall is NOT bytecode
  interpretation of the driver arithmetic; it is the serial un-fused FP64 cuBLAS
  op-DAG + per-op launch/sync dispatch (the structural wall the M2/M3/M5 closed-
  negatives already pinned). This RULES OUT "AOT-compile the per-step driver" as a
  path past the ≈3× cap. The remaining named levers are unchanged: a
  PRECISION/algorithm change (FP64→TF32/BF16 + dense compute-bound fusion) OR a
  right-sized GPU — NOT interpreter-elimination.

  SCOPE / what stayed (honest): the host-arithmetic driver glue WAS native-
  compiled and measured (the tractable, decisive slice). A full single-fused-
  megakernel step (which WOULD remove the per-op launch dispatch — the actual
  between-GEMM cost) is the separate megakernel axis, already closed structurally
  (#2697 + GN grid-sync) with its util term a distinct closed-negative. No
  interpreted glue was faked; the byte-eq gate held throughout.
