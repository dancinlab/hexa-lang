§FINDING — closed-negative (ruled-out axis)
────────────────────────────────────────────
  🔴 FALSIFIED. Precision-change does NOT uncap the flame CLMConvMoE step beyond the
  ~3× batch-fill ceiling. The low-precision tensor-core GEMMs are correct (TF32 rel_rms
  2.76e-04, BF16 2.61e-03, both inside the W14 ≤1e-2 gate; CE descends), but the STEP
  throughput barely moves: TF32 +2.0% / BF16 +2.6% at B=1, and TF32 goes NEGATIVE
  (0.967×, i.e. slower) at B=4. The util MEDIAN stays pinned at ~1% (≈0%) regardless of
  precision — the structural duty-cycle wall is unmoved.

  WHY (the mechanism this rules out): the FP64 cuBLAS GEMM is NOT the wall. The flame
  step is a chain of per-op launches (conv GEMMs + per-position groupnorm/gelu/residual/
  embedding/router/col2im glue) with the device IDLE between bursts (median util ~0% —
  the M2/M3/M5/INTERP-ELIM structural closed-neg). Making the GEMM arithmetic cheaper
  (TF32/BF16 tensor cores) shrinks the part of the step that was ALREADY a small,
  bursty fraction; it does nothing to the serial launch/sync dispatch + per-position
  host glue that dominates the wall. At B=4 the low-precision path is even SLOWER because
  the per-GEMM fp64→low-precision conversion + scratch alloc add launch overhead on the
  SAME under-filled, launch-bound structure — precision change TAXES the bottleneck it
  cannot touch. This is the same wall interp-elim (#2915, ~1.0×) and kernel-fusion-of-
  FP64 (#2910/2911) hit: the dominant cost is PER-OP DISPATCH, which is dtype-invariant.

  This RULES OUT precision-change (the g85-named candidate uncap lever) as a path beyond
  the ~3× flame self-speedup ceiling. It narrows the remaining uncap lever to RIGHT-SIZED
  GPU only (a device whose SM count matches the step's small per-op grid so the few
  bursts saturate it, raising the duty cycle structurally) — NOT precision, NOT fusion,
  NOT interpreter-elimination. The compute-bound-dense-fusion hope g85 floated is refuted
  for THIS step: lowering precision does not create a compute-bound regime here, because
  the step was never GEMM-compute-bound — it is launch-/glue-bound.

  HONEST scope: the gate is correctness-PASS (low-precision GEMMs are usable for the
  flame step within ≤1e-2 rel-RMS, CE descends) — this is a real, reusable artifact (the
  HEXA_GEMM_PREC tf32/bf16 path). The NEGATIVE is on the SPEEDUP axis only: precision
  does not uncap the step. A more optimal low-precision path (persistent fp16/bf16 device
  buffers avoiding the per-GEMM convert, fused into the megakernel) could recover the
  ≤2.6% and avoid the B=4 regression, but cannot change the launch-bound conclusion —
  the wall is structural, not arithmetic.

  VERDICT: 🔴 precision-change is CLOSED-NEGATIVE for uncapping the flame step. dtype +
  rel-RMS gate PASS (TF32 2.76e-04 / BF16 2.61e-03 ≤ 1e-2); step/s ≤1.026× at B1, 0.967×
  at B4; util-median unmoved (~0%); the ~3× ceiling is NOT lifted. The launch-dispatch
  wall is precision-independent → right-sized-GPU is the only remaining uncap lever.
