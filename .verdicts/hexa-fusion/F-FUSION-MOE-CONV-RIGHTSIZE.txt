FINDING (honest — right-sizing boundary, small-GPU vs L40S)
================================================================================================
BOUNDARY ON RTX 4070 (46 SMs) vs L40S (142 SMs):

• L40S baseline boundary: fused wins ONLY at d=512 (2.68x); loses for ALL d≥1024
  (d=1024 0.48x, d=2048 0.20x, d=4096 0.54x). ONE under-fill point.

• RTX 4070 (right-sized): fused wins at d=256 (8.91x) AND d=2048 (1.67x); ties at d=512;
  loses only in the narrow mid-band d=1024 (0.435x). The fused-favorable region is WIDER —
  it covers both the deep-under-fill end AND the large-d end.

WHY the small GPU widens the fused-win region (two distinct mechanisms, both honest):
  (1) DEEP UNDER-FILL (d=256, A:8 CTAs/launch): 8 CTAs ≪ 46 SMs → ModuleList catastrophically
      under-fills; fused 240 CTAs saturates → 8.91x. (On L40S d=256 the 8-CTA launch under-fills
      142 SMs even harder, but L40S baseline did not sweep d=256; the cure is most extreme here.)
  (2) LARGE-d LAUNCH-AMORTIZATION (d=2048, A:512 CTAs/launch): each of the 30 ModuleList launches
      is now big (512 CTAs > 46 SMs) so per-launch occupancy is fine — BUT 30 SEQUENTIAL launches
      on a 46-SM GPU incur 30× launch+scheduling bubbles between waves, and the kernel is HBM-
      bound. The ONE fused launch of 15360 CTAs removes all 29 inter-launch boundaries and feeds
      the 46-SM scheduler one uninterrupted wave-stream → 1.67x. This is the LAUNCH-OVERHEAD /
      WAVE-CONTINUITY win (boundary removal), the HEXA-FUSION thesis, NOT a faster conv. On the
      L40S the same d=2048 had 142 SMs to hide bubbles + better L2/HBM locality for 30 well-sized
      sequential launches, so fusion LOST there (0.20x). The SMALLER GPU has less bubble-hiding
      headroom → fusion's boundary-removal pays off → fused wins at large d on the small GPU.

  THE MID-BAND DIP (d=1024, 0.435x): the one regime where ModuleList's 128-CTA launches both
  fill 46 SMs AND keep good per-launch HBM locality, while the all-experts-at-once fused access
  pattern (naive per-out-channel MAC, no shared-mem tiling) loses on the memory roofline. Same
  roofline wall the L40S baseline hit — a kernel-quality axis (OG-FUSE-OPT), orthogonal to fill.

RIGHT-SIZING WIDENS THE FUSED-WIN REGIME: YES (measured).
  On the L40S the fused kernel wins in 1 of 4 swept shapes (d=512 only). On the right-sized
  RTX 4070 it wins in 2 of 4 shapes outright (d=256, d=2048) + ties a 3rd (d=512) = fused-
  favorable in 3 of 4 vs 1 of 4. A right-sized GPU keeps MORE of the d-sweep in the regime where
  the fused single-launch cure beats the 30-launch baseline — so right-sizing is the practical
  deployment lever: it dodges the big-GPU contention + the H100/H200 access-unresolved blocker
  AND structurally favors the fusion cure.

HONEST CLOSED-NEGATIVE COMPONENT (g5): the win is FILL + LAUNCH-AMORTIZATION + WAVE-CONTINUITY
  (boundary removal), NOT raw conv-math superiority — cuDNN's conv kernel = roofline, no raw-conv
  claim. The d=1024 mid-band loss (0.435x) is real: where ModuleList already fills the 46 SMs with
  good HBM locality and the launch count is small, the naive per-CTA MAC fused kernel loses on the
  memory roofline. Closing THAT needs a shared-mem-tiled / im2col-GEMM per-CTA kernel (OG-FUSE-OPT),
  a separate kernel-quality axis. The grouped anti-pattern (B) is NOT uniformly worst on the tiny
  GPU (it BEATS ModuleList at d=256 and d=2048 — even 30 channel-serial CTAs amortize launch
  overhead vs 30 separate launches on a 46-SM GPU) but it is never the best and reproduces the
  structural starvation at d=1024 (1230ms, 3.5x slower than ModuleList).

WHERE THE f5e18a0f WALL LIVES, RIGHT-SIZED: the production d=6208/E=30 shape on an H100/H200 was
  NOT measured here (access unresolved, out of scope — that is OG-FUSE-XOVER). What IS measured:
  on a right-sized 46-SM GPU the fused cure is byte-exact AND wins across a WIDER d-range than on
  the 3x-larger L40S — the right-sizing thesis (small GPU → more shapes fused-favorable) holds.
