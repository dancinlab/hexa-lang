════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ROOT-CAUSE ISOLATION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
The non-determinism is introduced EXACTLY when the device GLUE kernels engage
(groupnorm/gelu/moe-router/residual/embedding), and ONLY then:
  · host-only forward          → deterministic (Δ=0 over 3 runs, 17 digits)
  · + device naive-k own-GEMM  → STILL deterministic (Δ=0; the GEMM is NOT the culprit)
  · + device-resident glue     → non-deterministic (~7e-6 run-to-run)
So the binding term is NOT the GEMM reduction order (P1B already closed that) and NOT
the erf transcendental (clean at 9.3e-13, deterministic) — it is a RACE / async-ordering
non-determinism in the device GLUE path (the kernels share _forge_stream(); a missing
sync, an atomic-order reduction, or an uninitialized device-output-padding read in one
of groupnorm/moe/residual makes the per-forward result vary by ~7e-6). Because device-MEGA
AND device-EAGER BOTH route through device glue, BOTH are non-deterministic by ~the same
magnitude, and their EPOCHS=4 max|Δ| (3.3e-3) is dominated by that jitter compounding over
the step, NOT by any megakernel-vs-eager algorithmic difference.
