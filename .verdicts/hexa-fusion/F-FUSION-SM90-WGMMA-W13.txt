================= THE W12 ROOT-CAUSE W13 ATTACKED =================
W12 (#2851): the 128x256 output-tile axis is DEAD for this TF32 kernel; the real wall is NOT
occupancy but DECODE/MMA OVERLAP. In gemm_w10 the gmma decode scratch is a SINGLE shared band;
the per-slab sequence is  mbar_wait -> decode(composed shared->shared index) -> __syncthreads
-> wgmma.fence -> 4x wgmma -> wgmma.wait_group 0 -> __syncthreads.  The single band + the
wait_group 0 SERIALIZE decode(N+1) behind wgmma(N): the next decode cannot write the (single)
gmma band until wgmma(N) has fully drained it. W13 is the OPPOSITE of W12: KEEP the W10
decode-copy (W12 proved REMOVING it kills the kernel) and DEEPEN the gmma decode ring NSTG-deep
so decode(N+1) writes a DIFFERENT band while wgmma(N) reads band N — HIDING the decode latency.

================= THE W13 KERNEL (gemm_w13, MODE 6) =================
Producer-ahead software pipeline on the gmma decode band:
  - gmma scratch = NSTG independent bands (As0/As1/B0/B1 per band), ring-indexed ki%NSTG.
  - PROLOGUE: decode the first (NSTG-1) slabs into bands 0..NSTG-2 (no wgmma yet).
  - STEADY: for slab ki, FIRST decode the FUTURE slab ki+(NSTG-1) into its band (the producer
    half), THEN issue the 4 wgmma for slab ki reading band ki%NSTG (the consumer half). The
    future decode is issued BEFORE the current wgmma and touches a DIFFERENT band -> the
    scheduler overlaps the memory-bound decode of ki+NSTG-1 with the tensor-core wgmma of ki.
The swizzled-tile TMA ring (NST) is KEPT at the W10 value; NSTG is the NEW knob. The W13
question: does NSTG>=2 fit the 2-CTA/SM smem budget AND lift 70.7?

================= THE EXACT WALL (the closed-negative, deterministic) =================
The gmma decode band is 32 KB (2*64*32 + 2*32*64 floats = 8192 f32). The W10 frontier fits
exactly ONE such band at 96 KB/CTA -> 2 CTA/SM (H100 228 KB/SM: 2x96=192 KB fits, 2x128=256 KB
does NOT). Adding the SECOND band the overlap REQUIRES (NSTG=2) costs 32 KB -> 128 KB/CTA ->
2x128=256 KB > 228 KB/SM -> occupancy HALVES to 1 CTA/SM. The overlap is real and correct
(bit-exact, and NSTG=1 races precisely because there is no 2nd band), but at 1 CTA/SM the single
resident CTA cannot fill the SM's two warp-schedulers the way 2 CTAs did, and the ~26% occupancy
loss SWAMPS the decode-hiding gain (51.9 vs 70.7). There is NO smem headroom at 128x128 TF32 for
a second 32-KB gmma band at 2 CTA/SM. This is the SAME wall W12 hit from the other side:
  W12: shrink decode scratch -> 2 CTA/SM restored but decode<->MMA SERIALIZES (no overlap).
  W13: deepen decode ring     -> decode<->MMA OVERLAPS but 2 CTA/SM is LOST.
=> 2 CTA/SM and decode/MMA overlap are MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE for this 128x128 FP32-scratch TF32
   kernel. The async-pipeline-depth axis is closed: there is no NSTG that both holds 2 CTA/SM
   and overlaps the decode. W10's single-band 2-CTA/SM design is the local optimum on this axis.
