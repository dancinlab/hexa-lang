FINDING (CLOSED-NEGATIVE — the mma.sync warp-level axis is the ceiling, not the technique set):
  All three expressible cuBLAS-class mainloop levers (hardware-TF32 mma.sync, deep cp.async pipeline,

  (11.17 → 11.55 TFLOP/s) — a real but small gain that closes ~3% of the 30× gap. Crucially, the
  on-pod stage-depth sweep showed throughput SATURATES at 2 stages and REGRESSES beyond — independently
  confirming (a 2nd time, a different axis) that global→shared latency is NOT the binding constraint.
  The binding constraint is the warp-level mma.sync instruction class itself: on Hopper, cuBLAS reaches
  TC peak via wgmma.mma_async (warpgroup-level 64×N×16 async MMA) fed by TMA (cp.async.bulk.tensor),
  a DIFFERENT instruction class that mma.sync cannot reach regardless of pipelining/epilogue tuning. A
  warp-level mma.sync kernel — even a clean CUTLASS-grade one — tops out roughly here on sm_90 because
  it issues one HMMA per warp per step while wgmma issues a warpgroup-wide async MMA. The remaining
  ~29× is a wgmma+TMA rewrite (a different kernel, sm_90a-specific intrinsics), not a mainloop retune.
