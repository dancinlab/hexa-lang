     on the shared X) whereas the single fused launch interleaves 30 experts' weight
     streams across the SM array, thrashing L2 on the weight side. The fused kernel
     trades launch-overhead removal for worse weight-stream locality — a net loss once
     the GPU is already full.
   - i.e. the f5e18a0f cure (boundary removal) pays off ONLY where ModuleList UNDER-FILLS
