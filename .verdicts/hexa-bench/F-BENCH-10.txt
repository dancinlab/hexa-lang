================ ROOT-CAUSE (why fusion flips the cell where autotune couldn't) ==========

1. TRANSPOSE-ELIM IS THE BULK OF THE WIN. BENCH-9 ran a separate k_transpose that read+wrote
   the full M*K (2048*4096 = 8.4M-elem) A buffer every step purely to feed the bwd GEMM.
   Computing the bwd as A^T @ dGq via cuBLAS OP_T removes that whole pass (no buffer, no
   traffic) — exactly what inductor does by never materializing the transpose. This is the
   single largest of the per-step elementwise passes the GEMM didn't dominate at this shape.

2. FOLDED COPY + SINGLE-LAUNCH OPTIM trim the rest. The dGrad->dGq copy folded into the valley
   pass + the already-single-launch fused AdamW keep the elementwise launch count minimal,
   matching inductor's fused epilogue+optimizer.

3. WHY AUTOTUNE (BENCH-9) COULDN'T: BENCH-9 correctly proved the GEMM is identical (flame and
   torch ride the same cuBLAS algo) — so a better GEMM was a no-op. The residual was always the
   GLUE, and the glue is closed by FUSING flame's own kernels, NOT by touching the GEMM. BENCH-10
   confirms BENCH-9's root-cause attribution was right and acts on the lever it pinned.

4. HONEST: cross-pod torch.compile variance. This pod's torch.compile TF32 D=4096/B=8 = 0.9364ms
   vs BENCH-9's faster 0.7314ms (same H100/CUDA12.4/torch2.4.0, different physical host). Against
   BENCH-9's 0.7314 number, fused flame 0.8453 = 1.156x (still a small LOSE); against THIS pod's
   torch.compile it WINS 0.903x. The ROBUST, variance-free signal is the within-pod controlled
   delta: fusion cut flame's TF32 ms 9.7% (0.936->0.845) and fused flame beats THIS pod's
   torch.compile in all 6 cells. So the honest verdict for TF32 is: fusion closes ~40-70% of the
   ~1.28x BENCH-9 gap and the cell flips-to-WIN-or-TIES depending on torch.compile's run-to-run
   placement — i.e. flame is now torch.compile-PARITY at the single largest cell, within noise.
   BF16 (0.863x, 18.5% closed) and FP64 (0.994x) are clear, variance-tolerant WINS.
