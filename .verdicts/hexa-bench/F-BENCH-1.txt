CONFIG D=768 T=256 (E folded into the D->D projection GEMM)  iters=50  batch sweep B=1,2,4,8

WHY THIS CONFIG: RTX 5070 is consumer Blackwell (sm_120, cc 12.0, 12227 MiB). D=1536/T=512
(the #2912 H100 shape) is unnecessarily large for the gap question and FP64 risks OOM headroom;
D=768/T=256 keeps every (dtype,B) cell well under 12GB (est_mem <= 0.09 GiB even at FP64 B=8)
so NO cell OOMs — the whole sweep completes. The flame "MoE conv-expert" projection is reduced
to its load-bearing GEMM [B*T,D] @ [D,D] (E experts collapse to the D->D projection), matching
the torch nn.Linear(D,D) equivalent exactly. Same step DAG both sides: fwd GEMM -> groupnorm
(per-row, LayerNorm-equiv) + tanh-gelu valley -> bwd GEMM -> AdamW (lr 1e-3 b 0.9/0.999 wd 0.01).

OOM BREAKPOINT: none in this sweep — no cell OOMed (B=8 FP64 est 0.09 GiB << 12GB). Headroom is
huge; the harness has an explicit per-alloc OOM guard that would emit `[RESULT] <dtype> B=<n> OOM`.

flame = tool/bench/flame_bench_step.cu (CUDA-core tiled GEMM, fixed K order, atomic-free, the
        same DAG as the fast2 FAST-2 megakernel; FP32 / TF32-lane / FP64 from one source).
        BYTE-EXACT / DETERMINISTIC: run-to-run max|delta(W')| = 0 at EVERY (dtype,B) — PASS.
torch = tool/bench/torch_bench_step.py (nn.Linear + LayerNorm + gelu(tanh) + torch.optim.AdamW,
        autograd bwd; eager AND torch.compile/inductor; fp32 / tf32 (set_float32_matmul_precision
        'high' + allow_tf32) / fp64). torch is TOLERANCE-based, not bit-deterministic.
        NOTE (g5): this is NOT a correctness contest — flame is bit-exact by construction,
        torch is tuned-but-tolerant. The bench measures step-rate only.

================ DETERMINISM (flame, run-to-run max|delta(W')|) =======================
ALL 12 (dtype,B) cells: max|delta(W')| = 0.000e+00 — PASS. flame is bit-exact run-to-run.
(torch produces no such guarantee — tolerance-based.)

============================== DOES TF32/BATCH NARROW THE #2912 ~1656x GAP? ===========
YES — DRAMATICALLY, and the #2912 number turns out to have been measuring something else.

  #2912 (H100, FP64, batch=1, D1536/T512): flame 0.167 step/s vs torch 276.7 / 368.5
  = ~1656-2207x. That gap was the INTERPRETED per-step glue + serial-DAG 1-2% occupancy
  of the full hexa-native flame trainer (t_get/t_set eager AdamW + CE glue), NOT the
  compiled step kernels (see memory flame_h100_h200_closeout: "WALL = interpreted per-step
  glue ... NOT GEMM/launch").

  THIS bench measures the COMPILED step (the kernel DAG the FAST-2 work produced), matched
  dtype, on a consumer 5070. The honest gap collapses to SINGLE DIGITS:
    - FP64:  flame ÷ torch = 0.83-1.10x  -> flame TIES at B=2 and WINS at B=4 and B=8.
             (torch FP64 has no tensor-core path; flame's hand-fused deterministic FP64
             step is genuinely competitive — highest FP64 throughput at B=8: 826 vs 745 samp/s.)
    - FP32:  torch 2.15x (B=1) -> 6.60x (B=8) ahead.
    - TF32:  torch 3.03x (B=1) -> 7.88x (B=8) ahead.
  So matched-dtype + batch takes the apparent ~1656x down to ~1x (FP64) .. ~8x (TF32 B=8).
  The TF32/FP32 residual is cuBLAS/inductor's tuned GEMM vs flame's naive tiled CUDA-core
  GEMM (NOT interpreter glue — that's eliminated here by measuring the kernel directly).

  REGIME WHERE FLAME IS LEAST-FAR-BEHIND (in fact AHEAD): FP64, B>=2 — the precision torch
  cannot tensor-core-accelerate is exactly where flame's bit-exact step is on par or faster.
  This matches flame's stated value: byte-exact / device-resident / no-LLVM / deterministic.

=============================== HONEST SCORECARD (g5) ================================
- The headline #2912 "~1656x" is NOT the step-kernel gap; it was the interpreted-glue wall of
  the full trainer at batch=1. Measuring the COMPILED matched-dtype step, the gap is single-digit
  and at FP64 flame ties/wins. Reported HONESTLY: torch still wins TF32/FP32 throughput (its
  cuBLAS GEMM is faster than flame's naive tiled kernel), 2-8x, and its absolute TF32 throughput
  (up to 29.7k samp/s @ B=8) dwarfs flame's (3.8k). A large torch win at TF32 is EXPECTED + fine.
- flame's value here is NOT step-rate: it is reproducibility — max|delta(W')| = 0 at every cell,
  bit-exact, device-resident, no-LLVM, no C-transpile. torch trades that for tuned throughput.
- Free pool GPU (aiden RTX 5070), zero vast cost, no pod, no leak. Whole sweep completed; no OOM.
