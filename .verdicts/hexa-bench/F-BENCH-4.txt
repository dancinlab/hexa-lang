WHERE REAL OG10 LANDS (the BENCH-4 question)
--------------------------------------------
* Real OG10 lands BETWEEN naive and cuBLAS at EVERY batch, exactly as predicted (OG10 is
  6.09x off cuBLAS at the 2048^3 summit, so it cannot reach cuBLAS but beats naive once the
  GEMM is the bottleneck):
     ms/step:  cuBLAS  <  OG10  <  naive   for B=2,4,8   (B=1 OG10 0.137 > naive 0.113 —
     at B=1 the GEMM is tiny (M=256) so the wgmma launch/TMA-encode overhead per call costs
     more than the naive tiled kernel; OG10's advantage needs M to grow).
* The naive lane's gap GROWS with B (BENCH-1 signature): naive ms/step 0.113->0.688 (6.1x
  across B). OG10 is FLAT: 0.137->0.326 (2.4x across B). cuBLAS flat too (0.045->0.099, 2.2x).
  So OG10 inherits the tuned-GEMM's batch-FLATNESS — the 3-8x batch-growing naive penalty is
  REMOVED. At B=8 OG10 is 2.11x faster than naive (0.326 vs 0.688); cuBLAS is 6.93x faster
  than naive. OG10 closes ~36% of the naive->cuBLAS ms gap at B=8 (consistent with 6.09x-off).
* HOW MUCH OF THE NAIVE TORCH-GAP DOES OG10 CLOSE? On THIS H100 the sign FLIPPED vs BENCH-1:
  flame is FASTER than torch at this shape (ratios <1). The interesting axis is the 3-way
  SPREAD vs cuBLAS, not "closing to torch". The proxy was ~2-7x faster than OG10 (proxy/OG10
  ms: 3.03/3.12/3.27/3.29x) — i.e. BENCH-3's proxy was OPTIMISTIC by ~3x at this shape, and
  the real OG10 closes the naive->cuBLAS ms-gap by only ~30-40%, LESS than the proxy implied,
  precisely because OG10 is 6.09x off cuBLAS. BENCH-3's ~2x proxy ceiling OVERSTATES the real
  own-GEMM benefit.

TORCH-PARITY REGIME?
--------------------
* YES — and BEYOND parity. On this H100 at D=768/T=256, flame's per-step wall is LOWER than
  torch's for ALL THREE flame lanes at every B (flame/torch_compile in 0.06..0.89, all <1).
  This INVERTS BENCH-1's RTX 5070 finding (where torch's cuBLAS won TF32 3-8x). The reason:
  at this TINY shape (M<=2048, K=N=768, ~0.9 GFLOP/step) the wall is NOT GEMM throughput but
  PER-OP LAUNCH/PYTHON GLUE. torch eager+compile both floor at ~0.54-0.78 ms/step (Python
  dispatch + inductor's still-multi-kernel step), while flame's hand-fused CUDA step (one
  custom valley kernel + tight GEMM launches, no Python) runs in 0.05-0.69 ms. flame's
  own-GEMM (OG10) does NOT need to "reach" torch here — flame already WINS the step wall; the
  GEMM choice only sets the spread BELOW torch. HONEST scope: this is a small-shape, glue-
  bound regime; at large GEMM-bound shapes torch's cuBLAS would re-take the lead (BENCH-1
  5070 signature) — OG10 (6.09x off cuBLAS) would then lag cuBLAS/torch on raw throughput.
