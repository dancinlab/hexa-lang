F-BENCH-6 — CUDA-graph capture on the flame bench step: does it cut the residual ~2x?
=====================================================================================
STATUS: WIP (skeleton landed; aiden measurement pending).

QUESTION: BENCH-3 (#... F-BENCH-3) flattened flame's TF32 gap vs torch to a near
batch-INVARIANT ~2.0-2.9x and ATTRIBUTED the residual ~2x to "launch/glue/occupancy of
the serial DAG — a different lever (megakernel/graph-capture), NOT GEMM." That was an
ASSERTION, never tested. BENCH-6 tests it honestly.

METHOD: take the BENCH-3 cuBLAS-TF32 step DAG (fwd cuBLAS GEMM -> LN/gelu valley ->
transpose -> bwd cuBLAS GEMM -> AdamW) and wrap the per-step kernel sequence in a CUDA
graph (cudaStreamBeginCapture/EndCapture -> cudaGraphInstantiate -> cudaGraphLaunch) so
the whole step replays as ONE graph launch, amortizing per-op launch+sync overhead.
Measure captured step/s vs the un-captured (eager) baseline at B=1,2,4,8 on aiden (free
pool RTX 5070 sm_120, no vast). Source: tool/bench/flame_bench_step_graph.cu (eager and
graph paths from ONE binary). Driver: tool/bench/run_bench6.sh.

GATE (g5): graph capture changes NOTHING mathematically, so
  - max|delta(W')| graph-vs-eager == 0 (bit-exact), and
  - run-to-run determinism max|delta(W')| == 0.

RESOLUTION (the point):
  graph/eager > ~1.3x  => residual ~2x lived in LAUNCH overhead (graph-capture is the lever).
  graph/eager ~ 1.0x   => cuBLAS GEMM dominates the step; launch/elementwise is negligible;
                          residual ~2x is GEMM-THROUGHPUT (cuBLAS vs torch inductor-tuned),
                          NOT launch. (honest closed-neg pinning the residual to GEMM.)
