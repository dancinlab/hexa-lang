F-BENCH-11 — warp-specialized TMA producer/consumer pipeline on the OG10 own-GEMM
================================================================================
STATUS: WIP (skeleton) — GPU build/measure pending on H100 (sm_90a).

GOAL: push flame's OG10 own-GEMM (W10 composed-swizzle-decode TF32 wgmma, no-library,
no-LLVM, bit-exact, ~70.7 TFLOP/s @2048, 6.09x off cuBLAS-TF32) CLOSER to cuBLAS via the
W-ladder's known unexhausted residual lever = a fuller warp-specialized TMA producer/
consumer pipeline (dedicated TMA-load + DECODE warpgroup feeding wgmma consumer
warpgroup through DEEP smem multi-buffering / ring-staged DECODED gmma bands + mbarrier
producer/consumer sync). The canonical CUTLASS/cuBLAS Hopper GEMM structure that W10's
simpler single-shared-band K-loop lacks.

LEVER (gemm_b11, MODE 6): 2 warpgroups. WG0=PRODUCER issues TMA (cp.async.bulk.tensor)
+ does the bit-exact composed swizzle->gmma DECODE into a RING of NGM gmma-band buffers
ahead of the consumer; WG1=CONSUMER only runs wgmma over the ring band. Decode (smem
permute, no tensor cores) of slab ki+1 overlaps wgmma (tensor cores) of slab ki — hiding
the decode latency the W10 single shared band serialized. NSW = TMA swizzle ring depth,
NGM = decoded gmma ring depth (deep multi-buffering).

W7 PRIOR (CLOSED-NEG #2838): split the CONSUMER (2 wgmma WGs / 1 producer) found no lift
because the wall was DECODE serialization, not consumer count. gemm_b11 attacks the right
axis (ring-stage the DECODED band so decode overlaps wgmma).

GATE (g5): bit-exact rel_rms vs cuBLAS-TF32 ref (<=3e-3, ideally 0 vs proven W10 numerics)
FIRST; perf (TFLOP/s + cuBLAS-multiple, was 6.09x) only after. cuBLAS = ROOFLINE, NO
superiority fabrication. HONEST: a remaining large residual is a legitimate terminal
closed-neg pinning 'own-GEMM > cuBLAS' as the irreducible no-LLVM-purity frontier.
