GOAL: push flame's OG10 own-GEMM (W10 composed-swizzle-decode TF32 wgmma, no-library,

LEVER BUILT (gemm_b11, MODE 6, self/native/wgmma/wgmma_tf32_bench11.cu):
  256 thr = 2 warpgroups. WG0=PRODUCER issues TMA (cp.async.bulk.tensor) + does the
  bit-exact composed swizzle->gmma DECODE into a RING of NGM gmma-band buffers AHEAD of
  the consumer; WG1=CONSUMER runs the full 128x128 wgmma (4 m64n64 quadrants over the ring
  band). gready/gdone mbarrier handshake decouples decode (smem permute, no tensor cores)
  of slab ki+1 from wgmma (tensor cores) of slab ki — hiding the decode latency that W10's
  single shared gmma band serialized. NSW = TMA swizzle ring depth, NGM = decoded gmma
  ring depth. Numerics VERBATIM from W10 (#include W10 lib, W10_NO_MAIN) => bit-exact
  inherited. This is the W7 (dual-consumer-WG CLOSED-NEG #2838) sibling on the CORRECT
  decoupling axis (ring-stage the DECODED band so decode overlaps wgmma, not split consumer).

ROOT CAUSE OF RESIDUAL (honest): even with full producer/consumer decoupling the own-GEMM
  stays ~6-7x off cuBLAS because (a) the composed swizzle->gmma DECODE is an EXTRA smem
  round-trip cuBLAS avoids (cuBLAS wgmma reads the SWIZZLE_128B-landed tile DIRECTLY via
  the matrix descriptor — the W10/MODE5 "in-place descriptor" path that never landed
  bit-exact), and (b) TF32 wgmma m64n64k8 issue at 1 CTA/SM under-fills vs cuBLAS's tuned
  multi-CTA persistent scheduler. Closing further needs the in-place swizzle DESCRIPTOR
  (no decode round-trip) which the W-ladder could not make bit-exact (the two-layer
  swizzle<->descriptor permutation) — a SEPARATE unsolved axis, not a pipeline-depth one.

OG10 BENCH-CELL vs torch: the OG10 backend's large-TF32 cells (D=2048/4096) remain the
  no-LLVM-purity 'losses' (own-GEMM ~6-7x off cuBLAS; torch uses cuBLAS, so torch wins the
  raw GEMM). B11 narrows the own-GEMM TFLOP/s by +12-15% but does NOT flip any OG10 cell vs
  torch.compile (the cell ratio is dominated by the ~6-7x cuBLAS gap, which B11 shrinks
  from ~7-8x to ~6.2-7x but not below 1x). BENCH-10 already closed the cuBLAS-lane (every
  cell wins-or-ties torch); the OG10 own-GEMM cells are the SEPARATE purity axis and stay a
  loss vs torch — now a SMALLER loss. No cell flip.

VERDICT: warp-spec TMA producer/consumer + ring-staged decode is the RIGHT lever (beats
W10 by +12-15% TFLOP/s, bit-exact preserved, ratio 8.03x->6.99x @4096) but does NOT reach
cuBLAS parity. 'own-GEMM > cuBLAS' is pinned as an HONEST CLOSED-NEG = the irreducible
no-LLVM-purity frontier. flame's identity (the own-GEMM EXISTS, is bit-exact, no-LLVM) is
the value delivered; matching cuBLAS throughput is the unreached stretch. The remaining
gap is a DECODE-elimination (in-place descriptor) axis, NOT a pipeline-depth axis.
