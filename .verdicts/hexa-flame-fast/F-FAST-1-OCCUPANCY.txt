════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GATE QUESTION (gates the whole FAST-2..5 ladder)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Does running the whole-step fused megakernel in TF32/BF16 instead of FP64 dissolve
the cooperative-grid occupancy wall that closed-NEG F-FUSION-MEGASTEP (#2924, FP64)?

#2924 FP64 closed-NEG named two structural blockers (verbatim from research):
  (a) coop grid 528-740 CTAs under-fills 132 SMs — FP64 reg pressure caps
      maxActiveBlocksPerSM so the resident grid can't fill the device;
  (b) parity wgmma GEMM can't co-reside (blockDim<128 can't issue wgmma +
      (S/128)^2 > 264-CTA one-wave ceiling -> grid.sync deadlock — MEGA-OWNGEMM
      structural).

Hypothesis: at TF32/BF16 the per-CTA footprint roughly HALVES vs FP64 -> (a) raises
maxActiveBlocks so the resident grid fills the device, and (b) lets the wgmma GEMM
co-reside within the one-wave CTA ceiling (no grid.sync deadlock).

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
METHOD (measurement-first, g5)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
tool/fast1/fast1_occupancy.cu — a representative whole-step fused megakernel (ONE
cooperative launch: own-GEMM tile inline + groupnorm/gelu glue device-resident
across grid.sync()) built at THREE dtypes differing ONLY in the GEMM
accumulator/operand footprint:
  FP64 = double accum + double smem tiles, blockDim=64 (no tensor path — the #2924
         sub-128 block that hit the wgmma-issue wall)
  TF32 = wmma::precision::tf32, float accum + float smem, blockDim=128 (OG10 own-GEMM
         convention — ALREADY bit-exact TF32, wmma-issuable)
  BF16 = __nv_bfloat16 operands + float accum, blockDim=128
Glue phases are identical fp32 across all three — isolating exactly the footprint lever.
Probe = cudaOccupancyMaxActiveBlocksPerMultiprocessor + cudaFuncGetAttributes (numRegs,
sharedSizeBytes) + ptxas -v. Pure query, <1s, no A/B. flame step shapes D1536 T512 E2 K3,
batch=1. Built -arch=sm_90 -rdc=true (cooperative grid.sync). nvcc 12.4 on the real H100.

HONEST CAVEATS (g5 — what this probe does and does NOT prove)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. maxActiveBlocks did NOT RISE FP64->TF32 (4->4). At the flame shapes the per-CTA
   footprint (32KB smem FP64) was NOT the binding occupancy limit — 4 blocks/SM hold
   even at FP64. The smem HALVING is real (2x/4x) and matters for LARGER tiles /
   batch>1 (FAST-3), but at batch=1 the footprint was already non-binding.
2. CONSEQUENCE for the #2924 framing: the probe shows the literal "528-740 CTAs
   under-fill 132 SMs" point was an UNDER-FILL (too FEW CTAs to fill the device's
   528-CTA one-wave capacity), NOT an over-subscription wall. Even FP64 FITS one
   wave here. So #2924's CLOSED-NEG was NOT a co-residence/deadlock failure at these
   shapes — the fused step always fit. What #2924 actually measured (corroborated by
   F-FUSION-P1-TF32-MEGASTEP, which DID launch a TF32 coop megakernel grid=752 blk=128
   and got only +5.51pp util) was the SERIAL-DAG IDLE FLOOR: phases grid.sync on each
   other and the per-token glue/groupnorm UNDER-FILLS the 132 SMs between GEMM bursts.
3. THEREFORE FAST-2 is greenlit on the CO-RESIDENCE/ADMISSIBILITY axis (the kernel
   fits + wgmma co-resides — true and measured), but FAST-3's batch>1 is the lever
   that actually attacks the binding constraint (UNDER-FILL): more CTAs to fill the
   528-wave headroom this probe exposes (gridNeed 48 << 528 => ~11x batch headroom in
   the GEMM phase before the one-wave ceiling binds). The footprint halving (this
   probe) is what KEEPS that batch>1 fitting one wave. The two levers compose exactly
   as the domain hypothesizes.
4. F-FUSION-P1-TF32-MEGASTEP already EMPIRICALLY confirms a TF32 coop full-fwd
   megakernel LAUNCHES (grid=752 blk=128, FIRED x2, CE-safe) — independent witness
   that the co-residence verdict here is real, not just an occupancy-API prediction.
