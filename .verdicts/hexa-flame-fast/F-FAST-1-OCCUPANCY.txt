GATE QUESTION (gates the whole FAST-2..5 ladder)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Does running the whole-step fused megakernel in TF32/BF16 instead of FP64 dissolve
the cooperative-grid occupancy wall that closed-NEG F-FUSION-MEGASTEP (#2924, FP64)?

#2924 FP64 closed-NEG because (verbatim from reference_megastep_research):
  (a) coop grid 528-740 CTAs under-fills 132 SMs — FP64 reg pressure caps
      maxActiveBlocksPerSM so the resident grid can't fill the device;
  (b) parity wgmma GEMM can't co-reside (blockDim<128 can't issue wgmma +
      (S/128)^2 > 264-CTA one-wave ceiling -> grid.sync deadlock — structural).

Hypothesis: at TF32/BF16 the per-CTA footprint roughly HALVES -> (a) raises
maxActiveBlocks so the resident grid fills the device, and (b) lets the wgmma GEMM
co-reside within the one-wave CTA ceiling (no grid.sync deadlock).

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
METHOD (measurement-first, g5)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
tool/fast1/fast1_occupancy.cu — a representative whole-step fused megakernel
(ONE cooperative launch: own-GEMM tile inline + groupnorm/gelu glue device-resident
across grid.sync()) built at THREE dtypes that differ ONLY in the GEMM
accumulator/operand footprint:
  FP64 = double accum + double smem tiles, blockDim=64 (no tensor path — the
         #2924 sub-128 block that hit the wgmma-issue wall)
  TF32 = wmma::precision::tf32, float accum + float smem, blockDim=128 (OG10
         own-GEMM convention — already bit-exact TF32, wmma-issuable)
  BF16 = __nv_bfloat16 operands + float accum, blockDim=128
Glue phases are identical fp32 across all three — isolating the footprint lever.
Probe = cudaOccupancyMaxActiveBlocksPerMultiprocessor + cudaFuncGetAttributes
(numRegs, sharedSizeBytes) + ptxas -v. Pure query, < 1s, no A/B.
flame step shapes: D=1536 T=512 E=2 K=3, batch=1.
