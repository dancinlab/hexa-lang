THE COST MODEL (step 2b) — predict_tflops(mode,M,N,K,nst):
================================================================================================
  = PEAK_TF32(349) * issue_eff[mode] * occ_factor * wave_eff * drain_penalty * reuse_eff * fill_factor
  occupancy = MIN(smem-limited, REGISTER-limited) CTAs/SM:
    cta_per_sm = min( 228KB/(smem_per_stage*nst),  65536/(regs*256_thr) )
    -> the register limit is LOAD-BEARING: t256 = 154 reg/thr -> 1 CTA/SM though 96KB smem allows 2
       (the W11/W12 closed-neg). Modeling smem-only mispredicts the whole large-D crossover.
  issue_eff fit ONCE per kernel from D=2048 plateau (per-KERNEL const — OP-45 finding 1: D-invariant
    binary). D-scaling terms (drain/wave/reuse/fill) are then a PREDICTION at all other D.
  drain_penalty(K) = 1/(1 + c*log2(nks)/log2(64)), c calibrated so MODE8 nks 64->128 drops -9.9%.
  reuse_eff(TN): +8.5% @TN=256 (wider tile reuses A-loads). fill_factor: wide tiles need >=1 wave.
