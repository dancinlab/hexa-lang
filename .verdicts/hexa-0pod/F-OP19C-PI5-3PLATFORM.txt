GOAL
  OP-19 (#3002) + OP-19b (#3008) proved the flame CLMConvMoE step is machine-
  independent byte-exact on TWO platform combos:
    · x86_64-linux  (aiden, glibc)
    · arm64-macos   (local + ghost, Darwin libm)

  as aiden. If the deterministic byte folds match on pi5 too, the determinism is
  proven across 3 of the 4 arch×OS cells, not a 2-machine coincidence.

 5. BONUS FINDING — the libm CE-bwd divergence is an OS/libc effect, not arch
    (pi5 cleanly isolates this; STRENGTHENS the OP-19 thesis)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  OP-19 measured the libm `exp` CE-bwd fold diverging:
      arm64-macos 7969105254299072804  ≠  x86-linux aiden 3352931952497630952
  but could not say whether the split was caused by ARCH (arm64 vs x86) or by OS/libc
  (Darwin libm vs glibc) — the two machines differed in BOTH at once. pi5 resolves it:

      pi5 arm64-LINUX  CEBWD-LIBM = 3352931952497630952
        == aiden x86-LINUX 3352931952497630952   (same OS/glibc, DIFFERENT arch)
        != local arm64-macos 7969105254299072804 (same arch, DIFFERENT OS/libm)

  → pi5 (arm64) tracks aiden (x86) on the libm path because they share glibc, NOT the
    macos machine it shares an arch with. The libm `exp` divergence is a glibc-vs-
    Darwin-libm (OS/libc) rounding difference, NOT an arch (arm64-vs-x86) difference.
    The deterministic dt_exp/dt_erf folds collapse all three to identical bytes — the
    determinism removes precisely the OS/libc-dependent path this isolates. (Same
    GELU libm story: GELUFWD/BWD-LIBM are pi5-glibc values; the DET folds are equal.)
