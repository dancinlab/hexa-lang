for unbuilt modes / out-of-validated-range (the existing cases 1+2) but ALSO whenever:

i.e. NEVER launch a non-MODE-8 kernel on a shape whose pick was never GPU-validated.

(D=256/512), and those are ALREADY fallback-to-MODE-8 via case 1 (MODE_t64 is unbuilt on Hopper).

edit (re-tune / new mode / re-anchored coefficient) that extrapolated a NON-MODE-8 pick onto an
unmeasured shape would, without this rule, silently launch a kernel on a shape the GPU never
validated — exactly the honest-number error OP-63 closes. Case 3 makes that structurally impossible:
an extrapolated non-MODE-8 pick is always downgraded to the measured-safe MODE 8 path UNTIL a GPU
lane measures that shape (at which point its provenance flips to "measured" and the guard lifts).
