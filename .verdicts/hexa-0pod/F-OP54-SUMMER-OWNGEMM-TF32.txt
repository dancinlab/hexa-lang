============================ DOES OP-54 ADVANCE THE PARITY STORY? ======================
YES — it re-confirms, on a SECOND free consumer card (summer, was aiden in F-BENCH-5/OP-1),
that the TUNED sm_120 own-GEMM has CLOSED most of the original F-BENCH-5 gap:
 • F-BENCH-5 (raw OWN120 baseline, aiden): 3.16x-6.85x off cuBLAS-TF32 at S=768..4096.
 • OP-1/OP-1b TUNED OWN120 (the kernel measured here): the source's own measured note is
   "~1.0-1.12x off cuBLAS" — and OP-54 INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCES that on summer: median
   off-cuBLAS 0.95x-1.47x across the sweep, with D=768 + D=2048 at/under PARITY (own EDGES
   cuBLAS) even under 98% foreign contention.
 • There is NO small-D-closer monotone trend: the closest shape is the MID shape D=768
   (0.95x), NOT the smallest D=512 (1.15x); D=1024 is the worst (under-fill soft spot for
   the 64×64-tile kernel, contention-amplified). So the under-fill "simpler kernel loses
   less at small D" hypothesis is NOT borne out on the consumer card — the kernel's sweet
   spot is the mid-D regime where its 64×64 tiles fill the 50-SM RTX 5070 without the
   smem/occupancy pressure that hits 1024, and without the deeper K-drain that softens 1536.
