PER-LEVER VERDICT:
  (1) BK=32      — PLATEAUED/REGRESS. Doubling the K-tile doubles per-stage smem (As+Bs), cutting CTA
                   occupancy below the latency-hide payback at D=1024/2048 on the 5070. -1.7 TFLOP/s @1024.
  (2) 3-stage    — PLATEAUED/REGRESS. A 3rd ring slot is +50% smem for the same reason; the 2-stage
                   double buffer already hides the cp.async latency at these sizes. -1.7 TFLOP/s @1024.
  (1)+(2)        — INFEASIBLE on sm_120: 0xd200 (53.8KB) > 0xc000 (48KB) static-smem cap. Hard wall.
  (3) .v2 epi    — WIN (small, real, bit-exact). c0/c1 (and c2/c3) are contiguous in C (cols 2*tig,
                   2*tig+1) so they fuse into one 64-bit float2 store; halves interior-tile store insns.
                   +0.43 TFLOP/s / +1.7% @1024 (24.50->24.93, 1.143x->1.124x), small top-up @2048.
                   Scalar masked fallback at boundary/odd-alignment tiles preserves correctness.

================================== HONEST FRAMING (g5) ==================================
The residual was small (OP-1 already at 1.02-1.15x off cuBLAS-TF32) and the K-pipeline-depth levers did
NOT move it — they made it WORSE. On the RTX 5070's 48KB smem cap, the 2-stage / BK=16 config is the
occupancy sweet spot at D=1024/2048; deepening the K-tile or the cp.async ring trades occupancy for a
latency-hide that is already saturated, so it regresses, and stacking them overflows smem entirely. This
matches the OP-1 conclusion: the consumer-card lever is LAYOUT / MEMORY-INSTRUCTION VECTORIZATION, not
staging depth (the .v4 loads were OP-1's big win; the .v2 store is OP-1b's small one). The ONLY positive,
bit-exact lever was the .v2 epilogue — +1.7% @1024 (1.143x -> 1.124x off cuBLAS-TF32). It is shipped.
NOT regressed: BK=32 / 3-stage kept OUT of production (closed-negative on consumer card, logged).
