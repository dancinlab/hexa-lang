dx = "diagnostic-only fragile": the grep is DISPLAY-only (its stdout is piped
to the terminal/log), and the ACTUAL pass/fail decision is the next-line
artifact-existence check (`[ -s rt.o ] || fail`, `[ -x SMB ] || exit`). So the
GATE never silently passed — but the narrow mask SWALLOWED the real linker /
assembler error text, leaving an empty diagnostic before the failure, which is
exactly the confusing-failure half of the OP-129 class. Widening only ever
prints MORE real failure text → ZERO false-positive / CI-break hazard (a
display widening cannot flip a gate; the gate is the artifact/exit check).
