THE SHARPENED NOVELTY (doc §d, v2 — now TWO legs)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LEG 1 (unchanged, strengthened): cross-platform bit-exact training as a
  constructive recipe — now proven 4-architectures wide, including a
  non-backprop (STDP/Hebbian) substrate.

  LEG 2 (NEW — itself novel): the cross-ISA FMA-contraction nondeterminism
  class, measured + mitigated + bounded:
  - the CLASS: clang -O2 fuses a*b+c on arm64 (1 rounding) but not x86 (2
    roundings) → the FMA-fused farr_matmul kernel byte-diverges on identical
    inputs, reproduced ×3: F-OP29 (241449363 vs 1401117690) · F-OP31
    (2039553633 vs 124945498) · F-OP32 DIAG-A (1478294112 vs 210297454).
    Distinct from libm (T1) and reduction order (T2): a step using the kernel
    passes every layer-1/layer-2 oracle and still fails cross-platform.
  - the MITIGATION CONTRACT: determinism-path matmul = inline ascending
    reduction, formalized first-class (F-OP30-CROSSISA-CONTRACT); the inline
    rewrite of the SAME matmul is byte-identical cross-ISA in every case.
  - the BOUNDARY: binary {0,1} operands are FMA-IMMUNE (a·b exact ⇒ fused ≡
    unfused) — measured, F-OP32 DIAG-B (1881150137 on BOTH ISAs through the
    forbidden kernel) while the rate-coded twin diverges. Input-value
    property, not a kernel property; the contract RULE is unchanged.
