THE CONSOLIDATED CLAIM (verbatim)
  flame trains a real neural network (CLMConvMoE) bit-for-bit identically on ANY
  IEEE-754 platform — the same fixed-seed step produces the same weights,
  gradients, and loss to the last bit on x86-64 Linux (glibc) and arm64 macOS
  (Darwin libm), across architectures AND operating systems. This is a property
  PyTorch/JAX/TensorFlow do NOT provide: they route exp/erf/log through libm,
  which is NOT correctly-rounded (glibc vs Darwin round the last ULP
  differently). flame closes every such hole — the step has NO libm
  transcendental left; every transcendental is a fixed-iteration +−×÷ routine,
  bit-identical on any IEEE-754 hardware.

  Two layers, each independently verified:
   (1) run-to-run determinism (single machine) — per-op oracle series
       OP-2/7/8/9/10/11/12/13 + the end-to-end capstone F-OP15 (max|Δ|=0 over 17
       weights + m + v + loss).
   (2) cross-platform determinism (any machine) — close the two libm holes that
       survive on the step path: CE-backward exp (F-OP19) and GELU erf (F-OP19b).

DETERMINISM CONSTRUCTION (the reproducer recipe — §4 of the doc)
  (a) hand-rolled transcendentals, NO libm: dt_exp (CE fwd/bwd + GELU-bwd pdf,
      12-term scaled Taylor) · _moe_exp (MoE softmax, 14-term) · dt_erf (GELU
      fwd/bwd, A&S 7.1.26, branchless) · dt_ln (CE fwd) · _gn_sqrt (40-iter
      Newton) · _adamw_sqrt (24-iter Newton). Two distinct exp impls each
      load-bearing; dt_erf load-bearing BRANCHLESS.
  (b) sequential reductions — no tree/warp-shuffle/Welford.
  (c) ascending accumulation order (v/e/t/position/(t,c)/j-ascending).
  (d) fixed foldings (CE-bwd scale-then-subtract; AdamW left-assoc, ε outside √).
  (e) deterministic LCG init + no-uninit-scratch state threading.
  host↔device byte-eq: __device__ twins _hx_dt_exp_dev / _hx_dt_erf_dev.

HONEST LIMITS (§5 — g5, elephant rule)
  • dt_erf is 1.38e-7 from libm erf BY DESIGN (GELU fwd abs 2.11e-7, bwd ≈6e-9;
    CE-bwd dt_exp swap grad max rel ≈2.0e-14) — reproducible-everywhere, not
    bit-equal-to-a-reference-libm; ≤ GELU tolerance, training-equivalent.
  • TF32-mode is SELF-deterministic, NOT cross-precision bit-equal to FP64
    (training is chaotic; weights diverge while loss tracks). Cross-platform
    bit-exactness is an FP64-lane claim.
  • GPU determinism scope is single-machine here (host↔device byte-eq + cross-
    platform CPU byte-eq proven; cross-GPU-arch byte measure NOT in scope).
  • B>1 conv-seam intentionally non-zero: interior bit-exact, first (K-1)·dil
    positions per window>0 carry cross-window causal context (seam max|Δ|
    0.06–0.38 over 6 cases); platform-independent + run-to-run deterministic, just
    not identical to a segmented conv. (F-OP10.)
  • production-stdlib oracle final 0.0 read build-deferred (stale local hexa);
    self-contained twins + cross-language C byte-eq pin the re-lock.
