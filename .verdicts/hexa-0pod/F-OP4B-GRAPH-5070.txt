F-OP4B-GRAPH-5070 — CUDA-graph-captured flame FUSED step on consumer RTX 5070 (sm_120)
========================================================================================
STATUS: WIP (skeleton) — harness + driver landed; aiden measurement pending.

GOAL: OP-4 found flame's worst losses vs torch.compile on the consumer RTX 5070 are at
SMALL B (B=1), where the fused step's GEMM is tiny and the step wall is dominated by
per-launch + separate-cuBLAS-handle overhead (TF32 1.78x->8.96x as D grows; BF16 up to
14.66x @D=2048/B=1). OP-4b wraps the SAME fused per-step DAG (cuBLAS fwd GEMM + fused
valley + cuBLAS OP_T bwd GEMM (transpose-elim) + fused AdamW) in a CUDA GRAPH so the whole
step replays as ONE cudaGraphLaunch, collapsing that per-launch floor. Re-measure the worst
small-B cells (B=1, D={768,1536,2048}, TF32+BF16) graph-captured vs eager (un-captured OP-4
baseline) and vs torch.compile.

GATE g5: graph capture changes NOTHING mathematically -> max|delta(W')| (graph vs eager,
SAME snapshot+opt-state) == 0, run-to-run determinism == 0.

HARNESS: tool/bench/flame_bench_step_graph_fused.cu (FUSED step + cudaStreamBeginCapture/
EndCapture -> cudaGraphInstantiate -> cudaGraphLaunch), driver tool/bench/run_op4b_5070.sh.
