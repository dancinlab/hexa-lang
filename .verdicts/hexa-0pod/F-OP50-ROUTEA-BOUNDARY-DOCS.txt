The §0 statement, in 4 parts (all numbers traced to the verdicts above):
  (a) WHAT IT IS  — route-(a) pre-permute own-GEMM is bit-exact (rel_rms 0.000e+00 dev-vs-dev at
      every config — an equality, not a tolerance; the gate route-(b)/w16 FAILED at 1.107) AND at
      cuBLAS-TF32 PARITY @D=2048 (b14 MODE8 NST3 PDEP2: own ~315 TFLOP/s, ratio 1.08x, PARITY=YES
      = ~93% of the cuBLAS-TF32 roofline), no-LLVM / no-cuBLAS-call, device-resident.
  (b) WHAT IT ISN'T — NOT a cuBLAS beat. cuBLAS-TF32 is the roofline; 1.08x is parity-seeking.
      @D=4096 own falls to ~1.50x slower (own ~284 vs cuBLAS ~427, PARITY=NO) because route-(a)
      is SHAPE-RIGID (one fixed 128x128 plain-launch tile) vs cuBLAS SHAPE-ADAPTIVE (+24.6% @4096
      via large-tile/split-K/persistent; own -9.9% from 2x K-drain). spill/occupancy/D-indep-ptxas
      all statically EXCLUDED (OP-45). FP16 W14 ~11.5x off; W10 summit 70.7 TFLOP/s 6.09x off —
      neither a beat.
  (c) THE VALUE — bit-exactness + device-residency + no-LLVM-compile-theorem (a GEMM a persistent
      megakernel can call in-line where it can NEVER call the cuBLAS host API, end-to-end, bit-
      exact gate), NOT raw TFLOP/s-vs-cuBLAS. Mirrors the flame-side framing
      (project_flame_h100_h200_closeout): worth = correctness/residency/no-vendor-dependency, not
      step-rate. Do not pursue "beat cuBLAS" as a forge goal.
  (d) PATH FORWARD (IF a beat is ever pursued — NOT a standing goal) — the OP-49 shape-adaptive
      selector + cost model (§2-§6) + the 4 concrete config gaps (64x64 small-tile for small-D
      under-fill · MODE7 persistent measured @4096 · bit-exact tree-reduction split-K · NST-
      adaptive launcher), each a gated GPU-session build mapped to OP-45 T1-T5 (§7).
