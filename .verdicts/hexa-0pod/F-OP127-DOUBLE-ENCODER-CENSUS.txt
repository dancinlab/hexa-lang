1. THE TWO PRIMITIVES (runtime ground truth, self/runtime.c)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  hexa_bytes_to_str_raw(arr)  (runtime.c:6105)  — copies raw bytes verbatim.
       The registered builtin `bytes_to_str_raw`. CORRECT raw-byte→string.
  hexa_chr_byte(N)            (runtime.c:6067)  — 1 byte (N & 0xFF). RAW/CORRECT.
       `chr` LOWERS TO THIS under NATIVE codegen (codegen.hexa:7205).
  hexa_from_char_code(N)      (runtime.c:6035)  — codepoint N → UTF-8 (1..4 bytes).
       N>=0x80 expands to multi-byte. Over a RAW BYTE this DOUBLE-ENCODES.

  KEY ASYMMETRY (compiler/PLAN.md:3399 — DISSOLVED-BY-INTERP-RETIREMENT):
    `chr(b)`           → hexa_chr_byte under native codegen = RAW = CORRECT.
    `from_char_code(b)` over a RAW BYTE → codepoint re-encode = BUGGY in
                        BOTH the native and interpreter paths.
  ⇒ The true bug class is `from_char_code(rawbyte)`, NOT `chr(byte)`.
    The interpreter (where chr==from_char_code) is retired (@D g_interp_deprecated,
    R7 CLOSED); the shipping path is native-compiled where `chr` is raw-correct.
