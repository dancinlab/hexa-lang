THE BUG (iso8601_parse_native — the public input boundary):
  Contract says "Returns -1 on a malformed/too-short input" but it NEVER checked
  field ranges. Every out-of-range field was silently normalised through
  days_from_civil() into a garbage epoch instead of the documented -1 sentinel.
  A user round-tripping an invalid date had it silently corrupted.
