[4] SIGNATURE — adamw_step is NOT a drop-in for adam_step
---------------------------------------------------------
  adam_step  call sites : (p, g, m, v,    lr, b1, b2, eps, t)          9 args
  adamw_step runtime    : (p, g, m, v, n, lr, b1, b2, eps, wd, t)     11 args  (RFC 034)
  Differs by inserting `n` (element count) after v and `wd` (weight decay) before t — NOT a
  positional drop-in. A faithful repoint would have to synthesize n (array length) + a wd value.
  Since every adam_step call site is in a DEAD/never-passing surface (and those demos are also
  dead on the broader randn/zeros/slice/cross_entropy falsified-ML family), faithfully repointing
  them buys nothing → NOTE pointer comment instead (rule 3, mirroring OP-33c).
