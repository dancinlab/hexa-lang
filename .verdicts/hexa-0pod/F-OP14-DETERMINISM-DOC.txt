  1. THREE distinct exp impls, each LOAD-BEARING (a "unify the exp" refactor
     silently breaks byte-eq):
        libm `exp`  — CE BACKWARD grad        (F-OP11)
        `dt_exp`    — CE FORWARD loss (Taylor) (F-OP11)
        `_moe_exp`  — MoE router softmax       (F-OP8)
     (+ per-phase hand-rolled sqrt impls also load-bearing: _gn_sqrt = 40-iter
        Newton for GroupNorm (F-OP9); _adamw_sqrt = 24-iter Newton for the
        optimizer denom (F-OP12); neither is libm.)
  2. Reductions are SEQUENTIAL — NO tree / warp-shuffle re-association, NO
     Welford streaming mean/var. (F-OP8/9/11.)
  3. Accumulations are ASCENDING-order: softmax denom v-ascending; MoE combine
     e-ascending; CE fwd loss t-ascending; embedding scatter-add position-
     ascending; GroupNorm (t-outer,c-inner); conv/GEMM contraction j-ascending.
     (F-OP7/8/9/11/13.)

KNOWN NON-ZERO SPOT DOCUMENTED (B>1 conv seam, F-OP10)

  positions of every window AFTER the first (cross-window causal context a

  just not identical to a per-window-segmented conv at the seams.
