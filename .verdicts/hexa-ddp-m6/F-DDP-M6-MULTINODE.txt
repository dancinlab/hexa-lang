F-DDP-M6-MULTINODE — ring all-reduce across a node boundary (TCP socket transport)
=================================================================================
STATUS: WIP (skeleton pushed; loopback + 2-host runs pending)

Falsifier (g5): 2-node ring all-reduce result == serial elementwise sum,
byte-eq max|delta|=0 (FP64), for S%2!=0 (boundary, S=7) AND large S (1<<20).
The FP64 bytes cross the wire and must arrive bit-identical (TCP is reliable +
ordered, so byte-eq should hold; this proves it on real transport).

Method: stdlib/ddp/m6_multinode/ring_tcp_m6.c — the EXACT M1/M3/M5 canonical
ring schedule (2(N-1) steps, chunk_start partition), per-step transport swapped
from cudaMemcpyPeer (intra-node GPU, M5) to host-to-host TCP sendall/recvall on
a ring socket. Each rank is a separate process; rank r listens for predecessor
and connects to successor. Parity ordering (even send-then-recv / odd
recv-then-send) makes the 2-cycle deadlock-free without threads.
