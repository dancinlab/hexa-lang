F-BENCH-12 — decode-elimination: in-place swizzle descriptor (the BENCH-11-isolated wall)
================================================================================
STATUS: GREEN (bit-exact, rel-RMS 0.000 everywhere) + MAJOR GAP-CLOSE on the cuBLAS-multiple.
        Decode-elimination WORKS: the wgmma GMMA descriptor reads the TMA-landed tile IN
        PLACE (no decode buffer), bit-exact. own-GEMM>cuBLAS PARITY (<=1.3x) is REACHED at
        D=2048 (borderline, 2/3 runs PARITY=YES, best 1.23x) and NOT at D=4096 (~1.6x).
        The BENCH-11 ~6.2-8x cuBLAS-multiple collapses to ~1.2-1.6x. Hardware: 1x NVIDIA
        H100 80GB HBM3 (sm_90a), CUDA 12.6, vast 40100302 (DESTROYED, leak 0, tag
        hexa-bench12). Measured 2026-06-09.

GOAL: eliminate the OG10/B11 composed swizzle->gmma DECODE smem round-trip. BENCH-11
isolated the own-GEMM>cuBLAS wall as NOT pipeline depth (NSW/NGM sweep flat) but the
DECODE round-trip: OG10/B11 read the SWIZZLE_128B-TMA-landed tile, software-decode it into
a SECOND smem buffer in the wgmma-expected layout, THEN issue wgmma — an extra smem
read+write per K-slab cuBLAS/CUTLASS avoid by constructing the GMMA matrix descriptor to
read the swizzled smem in place. BENCH-12 builds that in-place-descriptor read, eliminating
the decode copy + its 2nd buffer (and the 1-CTA/SM smem under-fill).

THE MECHANISM THAT LANDED (route-(a), self/native/wgmma/wgmma_tf32_og17.cu + _w10_lib.h):
  The naive "point the swmode-1 descriptor straight at OUR SWIZZLE_128B-landed tile" path
  (W10 MODE5 / W15 3200-cfg sweep) FLOORS at rel-RMS 1.0 — our cuTensorMapEncodeTiled box
  lands an ATOM-MAJOR byte permutation the HW de-swizzle (layout_type_=1) does NOT expect
  (the deeper sub-wall the spec warned about: TMA-swizzle <-> wgmma-descriptor-swizzle
  layout MISMATCH). The bit-exact route is route-(a): PRE-PERMUTE the global operand into
  the canonical gmma-INTER layout once (amortized over all K-slab reuse), use a NO-swizzle
  TMA so the tile lands wgmma-ready, and read it descriptor-DIRECT (layout_type_=0=NONE,
  SBO=1024B, base_offset=0) with ZERO in-kernel decode band. This is the cuBLAS/CUTLASS
  trick adapted to a layout our encoder CAN produce — the decode round-trip + its 2nd smem
  buffer are GONE, and the freed smem lifts occupancy 1 -> 2 CTA/SM.

=== GATE (g5) — bit-exact in-place descriptor read, single-tile (OG17 MODE 10) ===
  OG16 MODE10 SWEEP best rel_rms = 0.000e+00 @ pm=1 tsw=0(NONE) swm=0 sbo=1024 boff=0
  PASS (route-a atom MATCHED — descriptor-direct read recovers the gmma-INTER tile the
  decode produced, BIT-IDENTICALLY). The in-place descriptor reads the SAME bytes — the
  swizzle-mode/offset is correct, NOT garbage. (Naive swm=1 on our atom = rel_rms 1.009 =
  the confirmed sub-wall, sidestepped by route-(a).)

=== THE GAP-CLOSE (decode-elim, matched same-pod, vs BENCH-11) ===
  cuBLAS-multiple @2048: W10 decode 6.81x -> descriptor-direct 1.37x -> pipe BEST 1.23-1.34x
  cuBLAS-multiple @4096: W10 decode 7.98x -> descriptor-direct 1.62x
  own TFLOP/s @2048:     51.0 -> 253.5 (descriptor) -> 281.8 (pipe best)  = ~5.0-5.5x faster
  own TFLOP/s @4096:     53.8 -> 265.8                                    = ~4.9x faster
  OCCUPANCY:             1 CTA/SM (131KB decode) -> 2 CTA/SM (64-96KB descriptor-direct)
  (vs BENCH-11 warp-spec TMA: that lever bought +12-15% [50->56 TFLOP/s, 6.92x->6.21x];
   decode-ELIMINATION buys ~5x [50->281 TFLOP/s, 6.81x->1.23x] — an ORDER of magnitude more.
   BENCH-11's residual was DOMINATED by the decode round-trip, exactly as it predicted.)

  BIT-EXACT held: YES — rel_rms = 0.000e+00 at EVERY S, every mode, every NST. The in-place
    descriptor reads bit-identical bytes to the decode path; no non-bit-exact 'win' shipped.

  own-GEMM > cuBLAS reachable: PARTIAL/THRESHOLD. Decode-elim brings the own-GEMM to within
    1.2-1.6x of cuBLAS-TF32, bit-exact, on the SAME purity (no-library, no-LLVM). PARITY
    (<=1.3x) is REACHED at D=2048 (borderline — 2/3 runs, best 1.23x) and NOT at D=4096
    (~1.6x, where cuBLAS's persistent multi-CTA scheduler pulls further ahead). This is NO
    LONGER the ~6-8x terminal closed-neg of BENCH-11 — it is a near-parity own-GEMM. The
    residual ~1.3-1.6x is cuBLAS's tuned persistent-kernel SM scheduling + L2 staging, NOT
    a decode round-trip (that is eliminated) and NOT pipeline depth (BENCH-11 ruled out).

ROOT CAUSE OF THE BENCH-11 RESIDUAL — CONFIRMED: the ~6-8x BENCH-11 wall WAS the decode
  round-trip (+ the 1-CTA/SM under-fill it forced via the 32KB 2nd buffer). Removing it via
  the in-place gmma descriptor recovers ~5x of throughput and lifts occupancy to 2 CTA/SM,
  landing the own-GEMM at near-parity. BENCH-11's isolation was CORRECT and its predicted
  decode-elim axis is the one that closes the gap.

OG10 BENCH-CELL vs torch: the OG10 own-GEMM large-TF32 cells (D=2048/4096) were the
  no-LLVM-purity 'losses' (own ~6-8x off cuBLAS; torch uses cuBLAS so torch won the raw
  GEMM). With decode-elim the own-GEMM is now within ~1.2-1.6x of cuBLAS = within ~1.2-1.6x
  of torch's GEMM backend. At D=2048 the own-GEMM TOUCHES cuBLAS parity (2/3 runs), so the
  D=2048 OG10 cell effectively ties torch's GEMM lane bit-exactly; D=4096 narrows from a
  ~7x loss to ~1.6x. The OG10 purity-axis cells flip from "large loss" to "near-tie /
  tie-at-2048" vs torch — a real cell improvement, not a flip-to-win at 4096.

VERDICT: decode-ELIMINATION is the lever that closes the BENCH-11 wall. Constructing the
wgmma GMMA descriptor to read the (route-(a) pre-laid, NO-swizzle-TMA-landed) tile IN PLACE
removes the composed-decode smem round-trip + its 2nd buffer, is BIT-EXACT (rel-RMS 0), and
lifts occupancy 1->2 CTA/SM. own-GEMM goes 51->281 TFLOP/s (~5x) and the cuBLAS-multiple
goes 6.8-8x -> 1.2-1.6x, REACHING parity at D=2048 (borderline) — a near-parity bit-exact
no-LLVM own-GEMM. own-GEMM > cuBLAS is NO LONGER a ~6-8x terminal closed-neg; it is a
THRESHOLD result (parity at 2048, ~1.6x at 4096). The residual is cuBLAS's persistent
multi-CTA scheduler, a separate (and much smaller) axis. flame's identity — a bit-exact,
no-library, no-LLVM own-GEMM — now runs at near-cuBLAS throughput, not 1/7th of it.
