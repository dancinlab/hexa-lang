F-BENCH-8 — close the FP64 large-D losses: add a cuBLAS-FP64 flame lane
================================================================================
GOAL (user-pinned): "모두 이길때까지" — beat torch in EVERY regime. BENCH-7 found flame
LOSES 7 FP64 cells at D>=1536 (up to 9.57x @D=4096/B=8), but ONLY because flame's FP64
lane used the NAIVE O(D^3) k_gemm instead of cuBLAS-FP64. torch's FP64 also has NO tensor-
core path (both sides use FP64 CUDA cores), so the loss is flame's naive GEMM being slow,
NOT a torch FP64 advantage. BENCH-8 adds a cuBLAS-FP64 flame lane (GEMM_BACKEND=5,
cublasGemmEx CUBLAS_COMPUTE_64F, CUDA_R_64F in/out) paired with -DBENCH_PREC=2, and
re-measures the FP64 cells D={768,1536,2048,4096} × B={1,8} vs torch-FP64 (eager+compile).

RESULT: HYPOTHESIS CONFIRMED. flame-cuBLAS-FP64 + no-Python glue re-flips 6 of the 7
lost FP64 cells to WIN. cuBLAS-FP64 now WINS 7/8 FP64 cells (was 1/8 with naive). The
ONLY residual LOSE is the largest GEMM-bound cell D=4096/B=8 at ratio 1.030 — a 3% hair
over parity where both sides call the same FP64 cuBLAS kernel and torch.compile's fused
glue edges flame's per-launch glue. Toward the goal: FP64 went from 1 win / 7 loss to
7 win / 1 (near-parity) loss.

================================================================================
WHY THE ONE RESIDUAL LOSS (D=4096/B=8, ratio 1.030)
================================================================================
At the largest GEMM-bound shape (M=2048,K=4096,N=4096) the D->D GEMM dominates the step
and flame + torch BOTH dispatch the SAME cuBLAS FP64 kernel (no FP64 tensor cores on
Hopper for this — both run FP64 CUDA cores at ~the same TFLOP/s). With the GEMM equalized,
the only differentiator is the non-GEMM glue: torch.compile fuses the groupnorm+gelu+AdamW
valley into the GEMM epilogue / fewer kernels, while flame still launches them as separate
kernels (k_valley + k_transpose + k_adamw). That residual launch/glue overhead is ~3% of
the 3.08ms step here, so flame lands at 3.1719ms = 1.030x. This is the SAME glue-fusion
gap BENCH-7 named as the irreducible residual on the cuBLAS lanes (TF32 D=4096/B=8 was the
sole battleground there too) — it is a FUSION axis (fuse flame's valley into the GEMM
epilogue), NOT an FP64-GEMM deficiency. cuBLAS-FP64 closed the naive-GEMM confound exactly
as hypothesized.

CONCLUSION: the BENCH-7 "flame loses 7 FP64 cells" finding was a NAIVE-GEMM artifact, NOT
a torch FP64 advantage. With cuBLAS-FP64 + no-Python glue, flame WINS 7/8 FP64 cells (6
reflips), each 3.3x-8.9x faster than its own naive lane, and the lone residual loss is a
3% glue-fusion margin at the saturated D=4096/B=8 — consistent with BENCH-7's TF32-cuBLAS
result. "모두 이길때까지" is now within a single fusion lever of complete for the FP64 lane.
