HONEST VERDICT — is "beat all" reachable?
================================================================================
ALMOST — and the residual is a known, narrow battleground, not a wall.

1. flame-cuBLAS (TF32 & BF16) is the goal-relevant lane: SAME GEMM as torch, so any
   remaining gap is glue/algo-selection, not raw FLOPs. It wins 14/16 cells; the only two
   losses are BOTH the single cell D=4096/B=8 (the most GEMM-bound + most filled). =>
   "beat all" is within reach by calling cuBLAS + the no-Python fused step; what's left is
   ONE corner cell where torch.compile's inductor picks a better cuBLAS algo/epilogue.
   Lever to close it: match torch's algo (cublasLtMatmul with epilogue fusion / explicit
   algo search) at large M·N·K, OR fuse the LN/gelu+AdamW epilogue into the GEMM tail so
   the step is one launch. NOT a megakernel/graph problem (BENCH-6 already refuted that).

2. flame-OWN-GEMM-OG10 (the no-LLVM-purity lane) loses at the large GEMM-bound TF32 cells
   exactly as predicted (6.09x off cuBLAS): crossover at D=4096 (B=1) and D=1536 (B=8),
   up to 4.44x. This is a SEPARATE axis (no-LLVM purity), not a speed regression of the
   stack — when speed is the goal flame calls cuBLAS (lane 1) and the own-GEMM loss is moot.

3. FP64 is flame's NAIVE-GEMM lane (torch has no FP64 tensor-core path either, but torch's
   FP64 cuBLAS GEMM still beats flame's O(D^3) tiled naive kernel at large D). flame wins
   FP64 ONLY at the tiny D=768/B=1 (0.162x) and loses elsewhere — driven by the NAIVE GEMM,
   NOT a torch FP64-TC advantage. CAVEAT: this is the naive-GEMM confound; a cuBLAS-FP64
   flame lane (CUBLAS_COMPUTE_64F) would likely flip most FP64 cells back to flame (torch
   FP64 has no TC either), but that lane was out of BENCH-7 scope. BENCH-4's FP64-win claim
   holds at small shapes; at large shapes the naive FP64 GEMM is the loss, fixable by cuBLAS.

BOTTOM LINE: "beat all" is REACHABLE for the practical (cuBLAS) lanes — flame-cuBLAS already
wins everywhere except D=4096/B=8 (1.27x TF32 / 2.00x BF16), one large-GEMM-bound corner
closable by inductor-class GEMM algo-selection or GEMM-epilogue fusion. The own-GEMM (OG10)
loss at large D is the EXPECTED no-LLVM-purity cost, orthogonal to the speed goal. flame's
identity (byte-exact max|delta|=0 everywhere · no-LLVM · theorem-cite · deterministic) holds
across all 32 cells. Remaining battleground = {D=4096/B=8 cuBLAS lanes} + the FP64 large-D
naive-GEMM (both GEMM-throughput, both have a concrete cuBLAS/algo-selection lever).
