================ ROOT-CAUSE (why autotune+epilogue can't flip this cell) =================

1. AUTOTUNE IS A NO-OP HERE. flame's GEMM wall is identical plain (0.9384 TF32 / 0.9066
   BF16) vs cuBLASLt-autotune (0.9363 / 0.9014) — sub-1% delta, in the noise. cuBLASLt's
   heuristic returns essentially the same kernel cublasGemmEx's CUBLAS_GEMM_DEFAULT_
   TENSOR_OP already dispatches for a clean 2048x4096x4096 shape. The GEMM was NEVER the
   residual; both flame and torch ride the SAME cuBLAS(Lt) GEMM. This REFUTES BENCH-7's
   asserted "inductor GEMM algo-selection" attribution for THIS cell.

2. EPILOGUE FUSION DOES NOT APPLY TO THIS STEP. The fwd GEMM's downstream op is
   LayerNorm (a row REDUCTION over the feature dim) BEFORE the gelu — cuBLASLt's
   epilogue only fuses POINTWISE ops (BIAS/GELU/RELU), not a reduction, and applying
   GELU in the epilogue would compute gelu BEFORE LN (wrong order, not math-equal). The
   step also has no GEMM bias (W is bias-free, matching torch nn.Linear(bias=False)).
   So the inductor-style "fuse bias/gelu into the GEMM" lever has NO fusible target here.
   The capability probe (HEXA_LT_EPILOGUE=gelu) runs and is correct as a mechanism but is
   slightly SLOWER (+2-3%) and off-by-order, so it is the WRONG fusion for this DAG.

3. THE REAL RESIDUAL = UN-FUSED ELEMENTWISE/OPTIMIZER GLUE. torch.compile nearly HALVES
   its own eager time (TF32 1.3204 -> 0.7314 ms; BF16 0.6811 -> 0.5212 ms) by fusing the
   LN + gelu + MSE-loss + AdamW elementwise traffic into a few kernels around the two
   cuBLAS GEMMs. flame runs them as SEPARATE naive kernels (k_valley LN+gelu, k_transpose,
   k_adamw). At the small glue-bound shapes (D<=2048, or D=4096/B=1) flame's no-Python
   fused-launch step still WINS — but at D=4096/B=8 the elementwise/AdamW traffic over the
   large 4096x4096 W tensor is big enough that inductor's elementwise+optimizer fusion is
   the decisive lever, NOT GEMM algo-selection. flame BEATS torch EAGER in TF32 here
   (0.711x); it loses only to the COMPILED elementwise-fused step.
