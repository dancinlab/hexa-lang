does shrinking the tile (the consumer-card analog of the Hopper-only 64x64 design) improve small-D?
=================================================================================================

QUESTION (HEXA-0POD OP-57): OP-53 (#3134) designed a 64x64 small-tile (MODE_t64) that the CPU cost
model predicts FILLS the device 3.1x better at deep under-fill (D<=512) on HOPPER. That design is
sm_90a wgmma — it does NOT compile on summer's sm_120 (wgmma rejected). On the consumer card the
in-tree OWN120 is ALREADY a 64x64 tile (BM=BN=64, 2x2 warps). So the sm_120 analog of "smaller tile
fills better at small-D" is: shrink OWN120 to a 32x32 / 1x1-warp tile (4x the CTAs/tiles at a given D)
and ask whether the more-CTA fill improves the small-D (D=256/512/768/1024) regime vs the 64x64
baseline — bit-exact preserved. This is the closest FREE proxy to the Hopper 64x64-fills-better
hypothesis; the wgmma 64x64 design itself remains a Hopper-only future build.

============================== METHOD (bit-exact-preserving tile param) =========================
The shipped OWN120 (self/native/mma_sm120/owngemm_sm120.cu) hardcodes BM=BN=64 / BK=16 / 2x2 warps,
each warp owning a 32x32 output sub-tile (WM_FRAG=2 x WN_FRAG=4 m16n8 fragments). OP-57 made BM/BN/
WARPS compile-time `-D` overridable in a CLONE (NOT a shipped-kernel edit), letting us build:
  BASELINE  = 64x64 / 2x2-warp  (the shipped config, -DBM=64 -DBN=64 -DWPM=2 -DWPN=2)
  SMALLTILE = 32x32 / 1x1-warp  (-DBM=32 -DBN=32 -DWPM=1 -DWPN=1) -> 4x the CTAs/tiles per D.
BIT-EXACT: in BOTH configs a warp owns exactly a 32x32 sub-tile = the SAME WM_FRAG=2 x WN_FRAG=4
fragment grid run in the IDENTICAL per-output K-major mma.sync loop. The 32x32 tile is ONE such
warp-sub-tile per CTA; the 64x64 tile is four. No reduction reordered, no split-K -> the result is
bit-for-bit the same. PROVEN by the gate: rel-RMS is IDENTICAL between the two configs at every shape
(below) — a hard confirmation the smaller tile changed only FILL/occupancy, not the math.

Sweep D in {256,512,768,1024}, iters=50, cudaEvent timing, 3 reps. flops=2·M·N·K,
off-cuBLAS = cuBLAS_TFLOP/s ÷ own_TFLOP/s (>1 = own slower).

================== ⚠ SHARED-HOST CONTENTION (g5 honesty — same as OP-54) ========================
summer is a SHARED free pool host (g9). Throughout the run the card was SATURATED by a foreign
sibling job (nvidia-smi verbatim: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070, 45% fan, 71C, 234.57W, 9430MiB/12227MiB,

kernels AND cuBLAS eat the same contention in the same instant, so the BASELINE-vs-SMALLTILE
comparison is the honest one. Absolute TFLOP/s reported as-measured, not extrapolated.

  S=256  rel-RMS=2.692e-05  max|delta|=1.037e-05  PASS  (baseline == smalltile, identical)

→ The smaller-tile config is bit-exact-tolerant CORRECT (rel-RMS ~1.3e-5..3.0e-5, same as OP-54's
  64x64). The gate is contention-INDEPENDENT (a numerical equality). The smalltile rel-RMS matching
  the baseline byte-for-byte is the hard proof that the tile shrink is a pure FILL change.

==================== (2) SMALL-D SWEEP — baseline 64x64 vs smalltile 32x32 (VERBATIM) ============
Median off-cuBLAS ratio over the 3 reps (raw 3-rep below). LOWER ratio = closer to cuBLAS = better.

  D     | baseline 64x64 off-cuBLAS | smalltile 32x32 off-cuBLAS | smalltile vs baseline
  ------+---------------------------+----------------------------+----------------------
  256   |   1.34x                   |   1.67x                    |  WORSE  (+0.33x)
  512   |   1.18x                   |   1.62x                    |  WORSE  (+0.44x)
  768   |   0.96x  ← own EDGES cuBLAS|   4.13x  ← collapses       |  FAR WORSE (4.3x worse)
  1024  |   1.44x                   |   2.52x                    |  WORSE  (+1.1x)

→ The smaller 32x32 / more-CTA tile is WORSE at EVERY small-D shape — most dramatically at D=768
  where the baseline 64x64 EDGES cuBLAS (0.96x) but the 32x32 COLLAPSES to 4.13x (own 5.6 vs cuBLAS
  23.3 TFLOP/s). The "smaller tile fills better at small-D" hypothesis is REFUTED on the consumer card.

WHY (physics, honest): the 32x32 / 1-warp tile has only ONE warp per CTA, which cannot keep the
mma.sync m16n8 tensor-core pipeline busy (the 64x64 / 4-warp config has 4 warps issuing MMAs
concurrently per CTA) AND it HALVES the A/B smem-reuse per global load (a 32x32 tile reuses each
loaded A-row across 32 N-cols vs 64 for the 64x64). The extra CTAs do light up more SMs, but on the
50-SM RTX 5070 the 64x64 tile already fills the device adequately at D>=512 (D=512 -> 64 tiles of
64x64 = well over 50 SMs), so there is NO under-fill for the smaller tile to cure — it only pays the
warp-underutilization + reuse-loss cost. This is the consumer-card MEASURED counterpart to OP-54's
finding that there is "NO small-D-closer monotone trend" on the 5070: shrinking the tile makes
small-D STRICTLY WORSE, not better.

============================ DID THE SMALL-TILE PREDICTION HOLD ON THE CONSUMER CARD? ============
NO. The cost-model's small-D fill prediction (OP-53 VAL-2: 64x64 wins 128x128 by 3.1x @D<=512) was a
HOPPER (132-SM, 128x128-baseline) prediction. On the consumer RTX 5070 (50 SMs, 64x64-baseline) the
analogous shrink (64x64 -> 32x32) goes the OTHER way: the 64x64 baseline already fills the smaller
device at small-D, so shrinking further only loses warp-occupancy + smem-reuse and REGRESSES every
small-D shape (worst at D=768, 0.96x -> 4.13x). The Hopper 64x64-fills-better hypothesis is NOT
transferable to the consumer card's already-small tile — and the wgmma 64x64 design itself remains a
HOPPER-ONLY FUTURE BUILD (sm_90a, ptxas-rejected on sm_120; OP-57 could not test it, only its sm_120
shrink-analog). The 5070's own-GEMM sweet spot stays the SHIPPED 64x64 tile at mid-D (D=768, 0.96x).

============================ VERDICT ===========================================================
🔴 RED for the hypothesis (honest negative): on summer's FREE RTX 5070 (sm_120, CUDA 12.9), shrinking
the OWN120 tile to 32x32 / 1-warp (the consumer-card analog of the OP-53 Hopper 64x64-fills-better
small-tile) makes the small-D regime STRICTLY WORSE at every shape (D=256 1.34->1.67x, D=512
1.18->1.62x, D=768 0.96->4.13x COLLAPSE, D=1024 1.44->2.52x), bit-exact PRESERVED (rel-RMS identical
to baseline at every shape, PASS). The cost-model small-D prediction did NOT hold on the consumer
card: the 64x64 baseline already fills the 50-SM device at small-D, so a smaller tile only pays
warp-underutilization + halved smem-reuse with no under-fill to cure. The shipped 64x64 OWN120 tile
remains the consumer-card optimum (D=768 0.96x, own EDGES cuBLAS — OP-54 reproduced here). The
Hopper-only wgmma 64x64 design (OP-53 MODE_t64) is UNTESTED here (sm_90a, ptxas-rejects sm_120) and
remains a future Hopper-GPU build item; the sm_120 shrink-proxy showed the more-CTA lever does NOT
help an already-small-tile consumer kernel. Free pool GPU (summer), $0, no vast, no pod, nothing to
leak; shared host left untouched (foreign PIDs 110588/483710 not killed) + temp build dir cleaned.

(The verdict is 🔴 for the SPECULATIVE hypothesis being tested, which is the honest, valuable
outcome: it CLOSES the "maybe a smaller tile helps small-D on the consumer card" question
NEGATIVELY with a clean bit-exact A/B measurement — saving a future lane from re-attempting it. The
underlying OWN120 kernel itself is unchanged + still 🟢 at its 64x64 sweet spot per OP-54.)

HONEST CAVEATS (g5): (1) absolute TFLOP/s contention-suppressed (98% foreign load) — the baseline-vs-
smalltile RATIO is the contention-robust comparison; both eat the same contention. (2) gate is the
established same-dtype TF32-tolerant check (rel-RMS ~1e-5 vs cuBLAS-TF32, NOT rel-RMS 0 dev-vs-dev;
cuBLAS-TF32 doesn't expose its accumulation order) — but the smalltile-vs-baseline rel-RMS being
BYTE-IDENTICAL is a true bit-equality between the two OWN configs, proving the tile shrink is a pure
fill change. (3) only the 32x32/1-warp shrink was swept; a 32x32/2-warp or 48x48 intermediate was not
(the 32x32 collapse at D=768 made finer shrink-sweeps clearly unproductive). (4) the Hopper wgmma
64x64 design is NOT measured here — sm_120 can't run wgmma; this is the closest free sm_120 proxy.

ISA one-liner: sm_120 + CUDA 12.9 ptxas — mma.sync.m16n8k8.tf32 (OWN120, both 64x64 + 32x32 tiles) =
YES; wgmma (the OP-53 64x64 design) = NO (sm_90a only; Hopper-future).
