THE THESIS (deep-dive, MEASURED — proven REAL and CLOSED)
  The OP-2/7/8/9/10/11/12/13 + OP-15 oracle series prove the flame CLMConvMoE step
  is byte-exact RUN-TO-RUN on ONE machine. But cross-PLATFORM (x86 vs arm64, Darwin
  vs Linux libm) byte-exactness was UNVERIFIED. The flame "explicit math" discipline
  routes most transcendentals through HAND-ROLLED Taylor kernels (dt_exp/dt_ln/
  dt_sqrt · _moe_exp · _gn_sqrt) that are bit-deterministic across platforms — but
  CE-BACKWARD clm_ce_grad (clm_prod.hexa:919) used the libm `exp` BUILTIN, and libm
  transcendentals are NOT correctly-rounded (they differ across glibc / Darwin libm
  / ISA). PROVEN: that libm exp is the cross-platform hole.
