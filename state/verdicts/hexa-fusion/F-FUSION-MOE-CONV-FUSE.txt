================================================================================================
FINDING (honest, fill-vs-roofline framed)
================================================================================================
There are TWO regimes, split by whether ONE separate-conv launch already fills the GPU:

• UNDER-FILL regime (small per-expert conv, d=512 T=64): each of the 30 separate launches is
  only 32 CTAs — far below the L40S's 142 SMs → the ModuleList path UNDER-FILLS exactly as the
  f5e18a0f root cause predicts. The FUSED single launch of 960 CTAs FILLS the scheduler and runs
  2.68x FASTER than ModuleList-30 and 2.85x faster than the grouped anti-pattern. THIS IS THE
  CURE — the structural 3rd option (fill via one saturating launch) that cuDNN cannot give.

• SATURATED regime (d≥1024): each separate launch is already ≥128 CTAs and the ModuleList path
  ALREADY runs at 98% util — there is NO under-fill headroom to recover on a 142-SM L40S. Here
  fusion loses: the all-experts-at-once memory-access pattern of the naive per-output-channel MAC
  kernel has worse L2/HBM locality than 30 sequential well-sized launches. This is the ROOFLINE
  boundary — the conv math is memory-bound and our per-CTA kernel is a plain MAC loop, NOT a
  faster-than-cuDNN conv algorithm (no raw-conv-superiority claim; cuDNN's conv = roofline).

The grouped anti-pattern (B) is the WORST in BOTH regimes (1.06x–8.7x slower than ModuleList),
reproducing the f5e18a0f "grouped conv defeats group-parallelism" regression cause: 30 CTAs
channel-serial can never fill the GPU.

WHERE THE f5e18a0f WALL LIVES: the H200 wall is the UNDER-FILL regime (each d=6208 ConvExpert
launch under-fills the H200's huge SM count). On the smaller L40S the under-fill is reproduced
at d=512; the cure (2.68x) is demonstrated there. On an H100/H200 the under-fill crossover sits
at a larger d (more SMs to fill), so the d=6208 production shape is expected to be in or near the
under-fill regime there — but that is NOT measured here (H100/H200 access unresolved, out of
scope). What IS measured: the fused kernel is byte-exact and DOES cure under-fill where it exists.

HONEST CLOSED-NEGATIVE COMPONENT: at the L40S saturated regime the fused kernel does NOT beat
ModuleList — the exact wall is that once a separate launch already saturates the SMs, fusion has
no fill to recover and a smarter (tiled-shared-memory / im2col-GEMM) per-CTA kernel would be
needed to win on the memory roofline. That is a kernel-quality axis, separate from the fill thesis.
