  RULED-OUT AXIS (the closed-negative, deterministic): on this native sm_90a TF32 composed-
  decode own-GEMM, the "larger output tile" lever (cudaforfun #1) is NOT a free win in isolation
  — it is OCCUPANCY-COUPLED. 128x256 forces smem 98KB->147KB/CTA, dropping 2->1 CTA/SM, and the

  consumer warpgroups"), never tile-alone. We isolated tile-alone and it regresses — the
  litscan's coupling is CONFIRMED by measurement, not assumed.

================= NAMED RESIDUAL (W12, next-cycle) ==========================================
  The 128x256 tile is correct and bit-exact but occupancy-bound. To make lever 1 pay off:
   (a) lever 2 — warp-spec register realloc (setmaxnreg producer 40 / consumer 232): the
       single TMA-producer thread already exists; split into a dedicated producer warpgroup
       with a register floor so the consumer WGs free registers, AND
   (b) SHRINK the 128x256 smem under 114KB/CTA — the decode scratch (2 A-bands + 4 B-blocks)
       is the swing; the W10-named "eliminate the software decode copy" (a wgmma operand layout
       matching the TMA atom stacking) removes the gmma scratch entirely (HW in-place RULED OUT
       in W10, so this needs a layout-emit, not a descriptor). Only with BOTH does 128x256 hold
       2 CTA/SM and the +34% reuse become visible. Tile-alone is closed-negative (this verdict).
