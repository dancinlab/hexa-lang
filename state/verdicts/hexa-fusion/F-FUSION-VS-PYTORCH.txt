§FINDING — honest, where flame ACTUALLY stands + the real lever
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. flame does NOT give ~1.3× on H100. At the production-proxy D1536 batch=1 it is
   ~1656× (vs eager) / ~2207× (vs torch.compile) SLOWER on the FULL training step.
   This CLOSES the "does flame/forge give ~1.3× on H100/H200" question — NEGATIVELY.

2. The wall is NOT raw GEMM and NOT kernel-fusion (confirming siblings #2910 CUDA-graph
   capture/replay ~1.0× and #2911 fwd+bwd fusion ~1.0×, both closed-neg). Evidence:
   the GPU peaks 100% during flame's GEMM bursts (util mean 8.70%, pct>=20 13.3% — SAME
   bimodal {100% in-GEMM, 0% between} profile as torch eager's mean 9.60%). flame's own
   GEMM is GPU-resident and ≈cuBLAS-class WHEN it runs. The 5.98 s/step is dominated by
   the time the GPU sits at 0% BETWEEN GEMMs — i.e. host-side serial glue, not GPU work.

3. The REAL lever the data points to (NOT kernel fusion):
   (a) The INTERPRETED per-step driver glue. flame's clm_prod step runs, IN THE BYTECODE
       INTERPRETER between device GEMM spikes: the per-window token t_get/t_set copy loop
       (B·Tw scalar ops), the ~28-call eager AdamW per-param tail, the CE/softmax-grad
       host glue, and the metric d2h. torch's equivalent dispatch is compiled C++ (eager)
       or a fused inductor graph (compile) — microsecond-class, not second-class. THIS is
       the ~1000× gap. Removing it = lower the per-step driver to compiled/native code
       (the M2 _adam_fused cooperative-launch is the start; the t_get/t_set window-pack
       and CE-grad host glue are the remaining interpreted hot loop).
   (b) batch=1 under-fills the 132 SMs (median util 0% — the serial-DAG occupancy floor
       from M5-BATCHFILL). batch>1 fills SMs but does NOT fix (a): the interpreted glue
       cost grows WITH batch (B·Tw t_get/t_set), so fill alone cannot close a 1000× gap.

4. HONEST framing: cuBLAS GEMM is roofline and flame's own-GEMM reaches parity at best —
   so flame was never going to BEAT torch on raw GEMM, and it does not. But the measured
   gap is ~1000× larger than any GEMM-precision or roofline effect could explain. The
   END-TO-END verdict is that flame's value is NOT step-rate-vs-torch (it loses badly);
   it is the byte-exact / device-resident / no-LLVM compile-time-theorem stack. The path
   to a competitive flame step is interpreter-elimination of the per-step driver, NOT
   more kernel fusion (that axis is exhausted: #2910, #2911, and this verdict).
