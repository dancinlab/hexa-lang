HOW FAR TOWARD GREEN
────────────────────
  GREEN threshold = ≥20% MEAN util. Full-step-mega MEAN ≈ 14% — short of GREEN by ~6pp,
  and the MEDIAN is 1–2% (the GPU is IDLE for the majority of every step). The +3.4pp
  is real but marginal; it does NOT approach the cited fwd-only 34.54% (that figure is
  not reproduced here — at D=1536/T=512 even the fwd megakernel alone leaves the host on
  the critical path between the few cooperative launches).

FINDING (closed-negative — ruled-out axis)
───────────────────────────────────────────
  Collapsing the WHOLE step (fwd + bwd glue + the entire optimizer tail) into a handful
  of cooperative launches — host fully off the critical path between micro-updates — is
  NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT to cross util-GREEN. The wall is the SERIAL-DAG OCCUPANCY
  FLOOR, intrinsic at batch=1 / D=1536 / T=512: each cooperative launch (grid≈528–660,
  one persistent grid) under-fills an H100's 132 SMs, and the residual per-token host
  glue + cuBLAS-shaped GEMM gaps + grid.sync barriers leave the device idle (MEDIAN 1–2%)
  between launches. Adding the AdamW-tail fusion on top of the fwd megakernel moves MEAN
  only +3.4pp because the tail was already a small slice of wall.

  This deterministically rules out "fuse more of the step into cooperative launches" as a
  path to util-99% at this regime. util-GREEN needs a DIFFERENT regime, not more fusion:
    (a) a larger problem (bigger D / true data-batching) so a single launch fills the SMs,
    OR (b) a right-sized GPU (smaller device where grid≈600 saturates),
    OR (c) a precision/algorithm change that turns the serial glue into one dense kernel.
  Consistent with the prior HEXA-FUSION closure (util-99%-via-megakernel: byte-eq ⊥
  util-lift; the remaining levers are precision-change OR right-sized GPU).
